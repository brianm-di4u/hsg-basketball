# Venues research: state-finals arena and Garland County gyms

Researched 2026-10-28 for the 3D scene art of Spa City Hoops. This file only gathers facts and
sources; it does not change `schools.json` or any game data.

**Confidence tags**
- **confirmed**: two or more independent sources, or one authoritative source (venue operator,
  school district, AAA, NPS, or a local news report that states it directly).
- **likely**: one non-authoritative source, a single photo, or an inference from sources.
- **unconfirmed**: sources conflict, or nothing was found.

**Image rule**: no images were downloaded or copied into the repo. The photo notes below describe
pages that were viewed in a browser, and each one links to its source. Do not trace or reproduce
these photos; use them only as reference for the generic art.

**Sources that could not be read**: pressreader.com (Sentinel-Record e-edition), arkansasbusiness.com
and loc.gov all blocked automated fetches (403 / Cloudflare). Any claim taken only from their search
snippets is tagged as such.

## 3. Generic Arkansas high-school gym conventions (art direction)

Short list for building believable generic gyms. These are general US/Arkansas high-school
conventions backed by the examples above, not facts about any one gym. Treat them all as **likely**.

- **Two eras of gym.** Older gyms (Glazener Gym, Irvin J. Bass, Red Devil Fieldhouse) are
  rectangular boxes with **pull-out wood or plank bleachers** on one or both sidelines, a **painted
  concrete-block (CMU) wall** in school colors, and a scoreboard on the end wall. New arenas built
  2015–2020 (Wolf Arena, Trojan Arena, Eagle Arena) have **individual plastic chair-back seats**
  (Wolf Arena: cup holders), a **center-hung multi-sided video board**, and an **LED scorer's
  table**.
- **The floor is the main identity piece.** Expect a light maple court, a center-court mascot logo
  (confirmed for Lake Hamilton's wolf head), and painted or stained lanes in school colors. Many have
  the school or town name painted along the baselines or sidelines (a common convention; not
  confirmed for these schools).
- **Wall dressing**: championship and conference **banners or pennants with years** on the end wall
  (this matches how `town-content.md` already hangs real title years), a **scoreboard at each end**
  (Wolf Arena: north and south), a pep-band section at one end (Wolf Arena: removable seats on the
  north end), and cheer squads on the baseline.
- **State finals at Bank OZK Arena** is a different look from a school gym: royal-blue arena chairs,
  a portable light court, courtside ad boards, and fans may not bring signs or flags. Crowd color
  comes from school-color clothing, not banners.
