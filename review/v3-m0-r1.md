## 1. Verdict

The packet shows a build that is more finished than `final-review.md` described. In this capture:
- the house is clearly dimmer than the floor
- a gold bead trail follows the ball
- the ball drops through a modelled mesh net with the baseline crowd jumping behind it
- the make slam sits clear of the rim on both viewports
- the slam no longer shows on an airball

Those gains are offset by what the richer evidence exposes:
- **Close and replay cameras** (c08, c15, c17, c18, c21) show mitten-handed mannequins with minimal faces, in front of a photographic crowd that is rendered at higher fidelity than the athletes.
- **Render artifacts look like bugs:** reflection wisps run under the white jerseys and even onto the dark apron, translucent ghost discs hover over the rim on every make close-up, and on mobile a gray box sits over the paint.
- **Overlays still land on the hoop and the ball:** the mobile "+3" sits on the rim, the close-camera slam card slides over the ball as it leaves the net, and an orphaned "+3" stays at the screen edge.
- **Unchanged:** the desktop camera still leaves 40-50% of the frame as bare wood, the menus are still cream web cards, and the replay back wall is an empty dark plane.

Net: **6.1**, the same number as the previous verdict for a differently balanced build. The gains in light, motion and payoff are cancelled by artifacts and character fidelity that the new cameras reveal. The gap to the stylized benchmarks (Hoop Land ~8, Playgrounds 2 ~9) is still about 2-3 points. It is mostly in four places: the character models, the lighting beyond plain hierarchy, the camera, and the front end.

### Rulings on disputed claims

1. **The crowd probe and `floorCrowd` (raised by Prosecutor A).**
   - **The bias is real.** In `probe/desktop/c02-live.png` the red crowd samples are a fixed grid. About half land on dark riser faces, seat gaps or black shirts, not on lit faces.
   - **My own check.** I measured the stills' whole crowd band against the floor. Using the area-mean crowd luminance, the floor-to-crowd ratio is 2.88 (c02), 3.10 (c05), 3.03 (c09 arena) and 2.91 (c11). Using the 75th percentile of the crowd (lit faces and shirts), it is 2.6-3.6.
   - **What `floorCrowd` is worth.** It reads about 10-20% high, but the hierarchy it reports exists (about 3x), and the pixels agree: c02 reads as a lit court under a dimmer bowl.
   - **Weight.** It establishes the 7 anchor's "clear hierarchy" and no more. It earns no credit beyond that, and it says nothing about ghosts or material quality.
   - **Probe action:** change the crowd metric to the area mean (or the 75th percentile) of the crowd band, so it stops sampling the gaps.
2. **"Faceless players"** (A, and `final-review.md`). Out of date. Desktop and mobile c21 show eyes, brows and a short mouth stroke, but no nose, and nothing reads at gameplay size. The 7 anchor is still not met.
3. **"No trail."** Wrong. The drive f13 zoom shows a chain of 4-5 gold beads from the shooter's hands toward the rim. The trail is real but sits on the crowd, so it adds little contrast.
4. **Desktop "+3" placement (Defender: "mostly above and left").** Partly. In c06 the plate (x 820-880, y 130-180) overlaps the left edge of the backboard (x ~870-900). It does not cover the rim.
5. **"Black voids" (Defender's rebuttal).** Split ruling.
   - **Not voids:** the gym aprons are dark wood with visible grain (c07 right).
   - **Near-black slabs that still read as objects:** the stanchion bases (c02 x 10-170, y 355-475; c06 x 1030-1190, y 345-470).
   - **True voids:** the replay back wall (c08/c17 x 540-1280, y 56-600, about 45% of the frame) and the arena near apron (c09 y 620-800).
6. **Probes that measure the wrong thing.** I agree with the Defender:
   - mobile c06 `floorCrowd` 0.86 samples the slam card and the dark lane
   - c15 `floorY` samples the apron under the bench
   - menu `floorY`/`crowdY` read the cards
   - mobile c08/c17 `playerPx` measures the replay close-up
   - mobile c06 `ballContrast` 1.01 is the ball inside the net
   - Mobile c05 at 1.02 **is** a valid flight measurement.
7. **Animation scores** (B and D 6.5). Too generous. The 7 anchor asks for a real net/rim reaction and a crowd that answers a make. The net bulges only mildly (net-close f05-f07), there is no rim movement, and the c07 airball draws the same raised arms as the make.
8. **Photographic crowd versus toon players** (A). Upheld as an Art defect: the clash is stark in c15, c18 and c21. Whether the crowd sprites are original art is **not shown** in the packet. It is not scored, but see the note under Blockers.

## 4. Blockers (must be fixed before milestone 1 can pass)

None is a regression; the build is unchanged. These are defects that read as bugs to any player, and the next milestone does not pass with them visible.

1. **Floor-reflection ghosts.** Pale vertical wisps sit under the white jerseys, including off the court on the dark apron.
   - **Evidence:** c16 x 820-940, y 330-690; c09 x 980-1000, y 590-650; mobile c02 x 370-470, y 1160-1260; mobile c06 x 120-160, y 990-1050.
   - **Pass test:** no visible wisp in a 2x zoom of c02, c06, c09, c16 and mobile c02/c06.
2. **Ghost discs over the rim on the make.** Four or five translucent white circles hover over the rim and glass.
   - **Evidence:** c18 x 480-800, y 115-210; mobile c18 x 160-620, y 300-460; net-close f00-f07 on both viewports.
3. **The mobile near-hoop box.** A translucent gray rectangle shows the rim through it, washes out the paint clump and cuts a defender's arm.
   - **Evidence:** mobile c02 x 330-550, y 935-1075; also visible in mobile c10 and c19.
4. **Callouts on the rim or the ball, and an orphaned callout.**
   - The mobile "+3" sits on the backboard and rim (mobile c06 x 340-470, y 435-505; make-wide f00-f11).
   - The close-camera slam card slides over the net bottom and the exiting ball (net-close f05-f10).
   - The "+3" stays pinned at the screen edge after the camera pans away from the rim (c16 x 1190-1230, y 85-125).

**Note (not a blocker, not scored):** the brief requires original art. The crowd sprites are photographic people (c15, c21). The packet cannot show where they came from. Confirm their provenance before the campaign invests further in them. Fix 3 offers a style change that would also settle this.

## 5. Ranked fix list (ordered by visual impact, covering the whole gap to the benchmarks)

### 1. Remove the render artifacts (Blockers 1-3)
- **Moves:** Lighting, Readability, Mobile, Animation.
- **Problem:** reflection wisps, rim ghost discs and the mobile near-hoop box appear in nearly every gameplay or make frame and read as bugs.
- **Evidence:** Blockers 1-3.
- **Fix:**
  - **Reflections:** clamp the planar reflection to the court polygon, fade it with height above the floor (`exp(-h/0.4 m)`) and a Fresnel weight, and cap it at about 0.12. If wisps survive at that cap, delete the reflection and keep the contact shadows plus a baked sheen gradient. The stylized benchmarks ship with none.
  - **Rim discs:** remove them, or replace them with one additive rim-colored ring flash of 120 ms or less. Drop trail positions once the ball is below the rim.
  - **Near hoop on mobile:** fade it to 0 alpha whenever its screen box overlaps a player box, or tilt the rig so it leaves the frame (see fix 8).

### 2. Desktop gameplay camera
- **Moves:** Readability, Art, Animation.
- **Problem:** 40-50% of every live frame is bare wood, the shooter's head sits in the crowd band, and the handler reaches the frame edge.
- **Evidence:** `emptyFgPct` 44.5 (c03), 51.4 (c07), 45.2 (c11); drive f08-f15 lower ~55% empty; `handlerX` 0.937 (c09) and 0.889 (c22); c05 YOU at about 405, 285.
- **Fix:**
  - **Framing:** lower the camera and bias the look-at toward the near side so the empty band stays under 15%.
  - **Handler:** add a follow dead zone that keeps the handler within x 20-80%.
  - **Shots:** pitch down 3-5° on a shot so the shooter's head clears the crowd line.
  - **Tests:** extend the safe-area test to live frames (`emptyFgPct` ≤ 15 and `handlerX` 0.2-0.8 in every desktop gameplay still and burst).

### 3. Character models, and one art style for players and crowd
- **Moves:** Art, Authenticity, Readability.
- **Problem:** the athletes are mannequins (minimal faces, mitten hands, tube limbs), and the photographic crowd out-renders them. The gap to Playgrounds 2 / Hoop Land's authored characters is the largest single Art gap.
- **Evidence:** c21 (face x 670-760, y 120-200; hands x 1000-1090, y 680-780); the c15 ref's stub arms (x 960-1010, y 380-430); c08/c17 #2; the photo crowd behind all of them. Benchmark: `benchmarks-04` playgrounds2-1 and -2.
- **Fix:**
  - **Bodies:** a nose and mouth, a stronger brow and cheek in the face decal, 3-segment hands with a thumb, tapered limbs, shoes with a sole, and 6-8 hair meshes. Give each player a silhouette that reads at the 130 px gameplay size.
  - **Crowd:** posterize and outline the crowd sprites (4-5 tones plus a dark edge) so they sit in the players' world. This also settles the provenance note.
  - **Close cameras:** keep them at waist-up or wider until the models are done.
  - **Clipping:** push the near plane out, or hide limbs within 0.4 m of the camera (c21).

### 4. Lighting beyond hierarchy
- **Moves:** Lighting, Art, Authenticity.
- **Problem:** the hierarchy exists, but the players are flat-lit, there are no light pools or bloom, and wood, glass, skin and cloth all render alike. That is the 9-anchor gap.
- **Evidence:** c02, c06 and c16 players have no rim light; c21 skin is one flat brown with no specular; the c18 glass reads as a gray frame; the scoreboard and exit sign do not bloom (c08 x ~410-425, y ~437).
- **Fix:**
  - **Players:** a key light plus a cool rim light on players only, through a light layer or shader term.
  - **Floor:** 4-6 baked soft light pools.
  - **Glow:** emissive bloom (threshold-based, half-res) on scoreboards, exit signs and the arena ribbon.
  - **Materials:** distinct specular for skin, cloth, glass and wood.
  - **Vignette:** about 15-18% at the corners on every tier.
  - **Budget:** stay inside 60 fps laptop / 30 fps phone.

### 5. Ball in flight and the in-world make payoff
- **Moves:** Animation, Readability.
- **Problem:** the apex ball sits on crowd faces, the trail is gold on gold, the rim never reacts, the crowd answers a miss the same as a make, and the jumper is stiff.
- **Evidence:** c05 at about 595, 113 (`ballContrast` 1.16); c04 1.10; mobile c05 1.02; drive f13 zoom (gold beads on gold shirts); net-close (no rim movement); c07 right-end arms up on an airball; jumper f01-f17.
- **Fix:**
  - **Ball contrast:** a 1.5 px dark outline on the airborne ball (the replay ball already has one: c08 at about 650, 178), a white-core trail, and a ~120 px screen-space dim of the crowd behind the ball. Target `ballContrast` ≥ 2.0 at the apex.
  - **Net and rim:** a net snap plus a rim flash on a make, and a rim shake on a miss off iron.
  - **Crowd:** stand the home section only after a home make; seat it or slump it on a miss.
  - **Shot animation:** add a knee load, a held wrist follow-through and a landing compression.

### 6. Callout lifecycle, placement and HUD polish (Blocker 4)
- **Moves:** UI, Typography, Readability.
- **Problem:** the "+3" sits on the rim on mobile and is orphaned at the screen edge, the slam crosses the ball in close cameras and covers transition players, labels are under 12 px, and the chevron scales with the camera.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 4; mobile c16 x 210-580, y 740-880 (the slam over a player); desktop "FOULS" at x 360-405, y 60-70; the c21 chevron at x 690-930, y 205-335.
- **Fix:**
  - **"+3":** float it above the projected top of the backboard, clamp it on screen, and drop it when the rim leaves the frame.
  - **Slam:** place it in the screen half away from the projected rim and ball, and fade it when possession resumes, by about 1.0 s.
  - **Bug:** shrink it to about 45% of the width, with every functional label at 12 css px or more.
  - **Chevron:** clamp it to a fixed screen size.

### 7. Front end: title, select, hub, pause, champion
- **Moves:** UI, Typography.
- **Problem:** the first and last screens are cream web cards with a generic sans, and select overflows 800 px.
- **Evidence:** c01, c13 (Quarters row cut at the bottom), c14, c20, c22 and their mobile twins. Benchmark: `benchmarks-02` top row (a consistent menu identity).
- **Fix:**
  - **Style:** a dark navy-and-gold broadcast panel style over the 3D gym, with the slam's condensed face for all headings and buttons and one text face with a defined scale.
  - **Select:** a two-step Home then Away select with large crests.
  - **Title:** frame the title wordmark fully.
  - **Test:** select fits in 1280×800 and 390×844.

### 8. Portrait rig
- **Moves:** Mobile, Readability.
- **Problem:** players are small, and defense shows the whole court.
- **Evidence:** `playerPx` 57.7 (mobile c02) and 47 (c11); the whole court in mobile c02, c10 and c11.
- **Fix:**
  - **Framing:** lower the pitch 8-10° and frame from the ball handler to about 3 m past the attacked baseline. Target 65-75 css px players.
  - **Keep:** the banners and end-wall crowd in the top band, and the thumb deck unchanged.

### 9. Fill the enclosure
- **Moves:** Art, Authenticity.
- **Problem:** the benches are empty, the stanchion bases are black slabs, there is no near side, the replay back wall is void, the arena near apron is black, and the Hot Streak crowd stands on black.
- **Evidence:** c15 x 20-650, y 280-520; c02 x 600-890, y 210-255; c02 x 10-170, y 355-475; c08/c17 x 540-1280, y 56-600; c09 y 620-800; c12.
- **Fix:**
  - **Benches:** 5 instanced bench players plus a coach per team, in uniform colors.
  - **Stanchion bases:** team-color pads with a lit top bevel.
  - **Replay wall:** give the replay-facing wall the far-side crowd and banners, or cap the replay camera's yaw.
  - **Arena apron:** a lit courtside row.
  - **Hot Streak:** a 3-step bleacher under the baseline crowd, and two ball racks.
  - **Test:** no near-black region larger than 3% of any gameplay frame.

### 10. Desktop place identity and the state-finals arena
- **Moves:** Authenticity, Art.
- **Problem:** desktop gameplay never shows a title banner, the final is the weakest venue, and school palettes are off.
- **Evidence:** no banner in desktop c02-c07, c09-c11, c16 or c19; c09 (no ribbon, "HOT SP" cropped); c02 (the Hot Springs crowd reads orange, not gold).
- **Fix:**
  - **Banners:** hang title-year banners on the desktop far wall, above the top visible row and below the HUD.
  - **Arena:** an emissive ribbon on the c09 fascia (y ~185-215), a fully framed apron wordmark, and a distinct arena crowd and seat color.
  - **Palettes:** map Hot Springs to gold plus black.
  - **Crowd:** a seeded no-repeat-within-3-seats crowd shuffle.
