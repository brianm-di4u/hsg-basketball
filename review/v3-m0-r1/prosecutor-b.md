## 2. Defects ranked by player exposure

1. **Persistent: portrait action scale and visual congestion.** `stills/mobile/c02-live.png` has a long empty route between the far basket and handler (x50–740 y310–700), while the near paint compresses bodies behind a translucent backboard (x260–580 y950–1180). `c19-handler.png`, x260–530 y930–1140, repeats that obstruction. Probe medians are 57.7 and 58.1 CSS px; `c11-mountain-pine.png` is only 47 CSS px, with distant defenders across x270–560 y450–560. The basket remains in view in these stills and the mobile jumper/drive bursts; the failure is scale and clutter, not an unsupported claim that it leaves the screen. **Fix:** frame the active half court more tightly, enlarge players toward 65–75 CSS px, and fade the near backboard more aggressively where it intersects players. Preserve the separate control deck: nothing important currently sits directly under the thumb controls in `c02-live.png`, bottom band.

2. **Persistent: defensive bodies read as a moving fence.** `bursts/mobile-open-play.png` f00–f15, lower-center paint, repeatedly presents several white jerseys with both arms spread in a dense row; `bursts/desktop-open-play.png` f04–f11, left-center paint, compresses the same action into overlapping limbs. `stills/desktop/c19-handler.png`, x370–550 y280–450, confirms the crowded silhouette at full resolution. This is insufficiently expressive spacing and contact; the strips do not prove precise foot sliding, so I do not assert it. **Fix:** separate bodies with contact constraints, vary defensive stance/arm height by ball distance, and show planted changes of direction and a clear contact recoil instead of sustained symmetrical arms.

3. **Every shot: ball visibility is unreliable before the trail becomes useful.** In `stills/desktop/c05-flight.png`, ball x595 y115, the orange ball crosses crowd faces; probe contrast is 1.16, versus 1.10 at release. Mobile `c05-flight.png`, x340–370 y520–550, reads against the crowd/apron boundary; contrast is 1.02 at 9.2 CSS px. A trail DOES exist: `bursts/mobile-jumper.png` f12–f17, center-left above the shooter, and `bursts/mobile-drive.png` f11–f15, center below the rim. It is therefore wrong to repeat the prior categorical “no trail” finding. **Fix:** keep a contrast outline and short trail from release onward, adapt their brightness to the background, and reduce background detail immediately around the ball. Probe ballContrast measures the ball against a surround, not the whole trail or temporal tracking; minimum 1.0 alone does not prove invisibility.

4. **Every possession: wasted desktop composition and edge pressure.** `stills/desktop/c03-gather.png`, y450–800, dedicates a large lower band to wood; emptyFgPct is 44.5. `c09-arena.png`, x1160–1280 y300–490, crowds the handler against the right border; handlerX is 0.937. `bursts/desktop-drive.png` f01–f03, extreme right edge, briefly pushes the attacker to the boundary as the camera catches up. The drive probe's handlerInBandPct is only 40%; its glossary does not define the band bounds, so that percentage is corroboration rather than a precise safe-area test. **Fix:** anticipate movement in the camera target and maintain a handler/attacked-basket safe region; use spare foreground for tighter framing.

5. **Every HUD view: secondary text is too small.** `stills/desktop/c02-live.png`, x360–405 y60–70, renders FOULS at roughly ten pixels in the 1280-wide image. Mobile `c02-live.png` FOULS is about 20 native pixels tall, hence about ten CSS pixels; STEAL and JUMP in the bottom control deck are also below 12 CSS px. Main scores remain readable at the top. **Fix:** enforce a 12 CSS px minimum for functional text, simplify secondary labels if necessary, and spend less width on empty foul dots. This is a pixel estimate, not a claimed font-size inspection.

6. **Every make: the celebration follows the camera into live traffic.** `bursts/mobile-make-wide.png` f03–f07, center-left, brings the 3 POINTS plate across the controlled player's upper body; f08–f13, lower-center, overlaps transition traffic. `stills/mobile/c16-crowd-reaction.png`, x210–580 y740–880, confirms a player mostly hidden behind it 0.8 seconds after the make. The plate stays through all 14 burst frames, spanning about 1.08 simulated seconds. The rim is separate from the main plate in `c06-make.png` (rim near x405 y510, plate y750–860), but +3 sits over the backboard (x355–455 y440–505). **Fix:** anchor the plate to a reserved screen margin, shorten it as possession resumes, and place +3 above the glass rather than across it. Preserve its strong condensed face and gold underline.

7. **Each shot payoff: motion exists but remains mechanical.** `bursts/desktop-jumper.png` f01–f07, shooter upper-right/upper-center, shows lift and arm elevation; f08–f17 returns him to the floor. `bursts/mobile-jumper.png` f08–f10, center-left, shows the ball separated from the hands. These are real stages, but the broad raised-arm release and quick return lack a distinctive held shooting wrist or weighted landing. `bursts/desktop-net-close.png` f00–f07, central net, shows changing net shape and the ball emerging below; rim deflection is too subtle here to certify. Crowd arms are already up in `bursts/mobile-drive.png` f00, upper baseline, and remain up during `mobile-make-wide.png` f00–f13; a make-specific crowd response is not demonstrated. **Fix:** add a clearer knee load, wrist follow-through and landing compression, then trigger a distinct crowd wave after the net event. Do not replace the visible ball-through-net beat with more overlay effects.

8. **Menus on entry and setup: form-like presentation and below-fold setup.** `stills/mobile/c01-title.png`, x20–760 y245–1410, uses a cream card and stacked white buttons; `c13-select.png`, y1200–1688, reaches only partway through Away team choices. Desktop `c13-select.png`, y770–800, cuts the Quarters controls. The image does not establish whether scrolling works, so this is a fit/discoverability defect, not a claim that setup is impossible. **Fix:** split home/away selection into two compact steps, keep the start action visible, and unify menus with the dark/gold broadcast presentation. Preserve clear contrast and selected-state outlines.
