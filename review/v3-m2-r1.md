## 1. Verdict

This is a real step up from 6.1, and most of it is exactly what the m0 verdict asked for:
- All the m0 artifacts are gone or nearly gone: no reflection wisps, no ring of ghost discs, no near-hoop box.
- The airborne ball now has an outline and a dimming halo behind it (`ballContrast` 5.02 at c04/c05 and 4.37 on mobile c05, up from 1.10/1.16/1.02).
- The desktop camera fills the frame (`emptyFgPct` 6-12.5 in most live stills, down from 44-51), and mobile players are 80-88 css px in c02-c07 (up from 47-58).
- The "+3" floats above the board on both viewports, the chevron is clamped, the replay wall is built, the stanchions are padded, and the front end is one navy-and-gold broadcast style.

What holds the score at 6.5 rather than 7:
- **The new effects leave artifacts of their own:**
  - The ball-dim halo paints a dark blob on the floor (make-wide f04-f06).
  - A pale disc still covers the ball inside the net (c18, net-close f00-f04).
  - The grade jumps mid-play (open-play f13 to f14).
  - A red sliver floats over the mobile court (mobile c02, c19).
- **The slam card still sits on the ball and on the controlled player** (c06; make-wide f07).
- **The replay lost its subject:** no shooter, and the ball is clipped by the letterbox (c08, c17).
- **Select still overflows** on both viewports (c13).
- **The athletes are the same mannequins**, in front of a photographic crowd that now fills about 45% of every desktop live frame and visibly repeats.

Against the milestone targets, Lighting (6.5) and Animation (6.5) pass. Overall (6.5), Readability (7.0) and UI (6.5) miss. There are 4 blockers.

### Rulings on disputed claims

1. **The ghost disc over the rim: "closed" (B, D) or "one disc remains" (A)?** Upheld for A.
   - In my 3x zoom of c18, the net strands in front of the ball are near-white inside a crisp circle about 140 px across (x 570-710, y 240-380). Elsewhere the net is mid-grey, and the orange ball (`ballPx` 109) only shows through the holes.
   - The same pale disc shows in net-close f00-f04 and in mobile c18.
   - The 4-5 floating discs of m0 are gone. One additive disc is still drawn over the ball. Blocker 2 of m0 is **Partly** closed.
2. **Bloom: "on the scoreboard and shot clocks" (A) or "no visible bloom" (D)?** Upheld for A, but it is subtle.
   - In c08 at y 130, the "1" of "1:58" peaks at 255 (x 580-582) and falls to the board's background over about 8 px on each side (x 572-590).
   - That is a glow halo, not anti-aliasing. It is confined to emissive digits. There are no lamps or pools.
   - It earns the 7 anchor's "no gloss" floor, not the 9 anchor.
3. **The floor smudge around the ball (A).** Upheld.
   - My 3x zooms of make-wide f05 and f06 show a dark radial halo about three ball-diameters wide around the rolling ball, on bright wood.
   - It is the crowd-dim halo applied over the floor. It reads as dirt following the ball.
4. **The grade pop (A; D's concession).** Upheld.
   - Open-play f14 and f15 are visibly paler and flatter than f00-f13, on the floor and the crowd alike, across a 4-tick step.
   - The cause is not shown.
5. **"The replay scoreboard shows the wrong teams" (D).** Not established, and not scored.
   - Before-after `desktop-04` shows the replay in the Cobras gym in both rounds, so the replay capture comes from a different game than c06.
   - **Upheld instead:** the replay frames no shooter, and the ball is clipped by the top letterbox (c08 x 395-460, y 56-80; before-after `desktop-04`, where m0 framed #2 and the ball). Mobile c17 has no ball and a basket half off the right edge.
6. **Crowd repeats (A).** Upheld. The same clapping blonde sprite in an orange top recurs many times across c02 x 380-1000, y 90-300, including in neighbouring seats.
7. **Benches: "spectators" (A) or "empty" (B, D)?** Both are partly right, and neither is a team bench.
   - In c02 (x 575-760, y 290-355) street-clothes crowd sprites sit in the bench row between empty orange chairs.
   - The c15 chairs are empty.
8. **Desktop "FOULS" size.** Borderline, not a defect on its own. A cap height of about 9 px implies roughly a 12-13 px font. The mobile STEAL/JUMP labels are clearly under 12 px.
9. **Mobile aliasing (A).** Upheld as minor. My 2x nearest-neighbour zoom of mobile c11 shows stair-steps on the YOU ring and the white jersey edge. At phone DPR it is a polish item, not a blocker.
10. **c11 desktop `playerPx` 207 (A).** Agreed. c11 frames only two players (sheet `desktop-02`), so it does not show that the live camera holds 10 players at that size.
11. **Probes that measure the wrong thing:**
    - c08, c17 and c15 `floorCrowd` measure close or replay framing.
    - Mobile c08/c17 `ballContrast` 1.0 is a letterboxed or cropped ball.
    - Menu `floorY`/`crowdY` read the panels.
    - The c05 `ballContrast` 5.02 does include the dark halo, but that halo is real pixels (my c05 zoom), so it is a valid measure.

## 5. Ranked fix list (ordered by visual impact)

### 1. Clear the artifacts (Blocker 2)
- **Moves:** Lighting, Animation, Readability, Mobile.
- **Problem:** the ball-dim halo and the leftover make halo now create the artifacts the last milestone removed.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 2.
- **Fix:**
  - **Crowd-dim halo:** apply it only where the ball's screen position is above the projected court edge, using a crowd or depth mask. Fade it out over 100 ms when the ball drops below the rim plane.
  - **Make halo:** remove the additive disc or sprite once the ball is inside the net. Keep the rim flash.
  - **Red sliver:** delete the near-hoop remnant (or set it invisible, not just the box).
  - **Grade pop:** if a quality tier switches, lock the grade, tonemap and bloom across tiers. Switch only at dead balls, or cross-fade over 0.5 s.

### 2. Callout placement and lifecycle (Blocker 1)
- **Moves:** UI, Readability, Animation.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 1.
- **Fix:**
  - Choose the card's slot once, at spawn, from candidate slots tested against the projected ball, rim and all player boxes (plus 24 px).
  - Never move it after spawn.
  - Fade it on possession resumption, by 1.0 s at most.
  - On desktop, drop the card about 60 px when the projected rim sits low in frame (c06).

### 3. Replay camera (Blocker 3)
- **Moves:** Animation, UI, Readability.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 3.
- **Fix:**
  - Frame the shooter at release and the ball's arc to the rim, keeping the new built wall as the background.
  - Clamp the camera so the ball's projected position stays 40 px inside the letterbox.
  - Make "early" and "late" visibly different beats: release, then the ball through the net.

### 4. Characters, and one art style with the crowd
- **Moves:** Art, Authenticity, Readability.
- **Problem:** the largest remaining Art gap. The mannequins sit against a photo crowd that now fills about 45% of the frame and repeats.
- **Evidence:**
  - c21: faces x 430-810, y 110-290; hand x 1050-1130, y 640-770; shard x 405-520, y 385-640; near-plane limb x 0-300, y 160-430.
  - The c02 crowd repeats.
  - Benchmark sheet 04 (Playgrounds 2).
- **Fix:**
  - **Players:** as m0 fix 3 (a nose and mouth, 3-segment hands with a thumb, tapered limbs, soled shoes, 6-8 hair meshes). Bind the number decal to the torso. Hide meshes within 0.4 m of the camera.
  - **Crowd:** posterize to 4-5 tones with a dark edge and desaturate 20-30%. Use a seeded no-repeat-within-3-seats shuffle with a mirror and ±8% tint jitter.
  - **Close cameras:** keep them at waist-up or wider until the models land.

### 5. Camera in motion
- **Moves:** Readability, Mobile.
- **Evidence:**
  - Desktop drive f00-f01: the handler is off the frame or at the edge (`handlerInBandPct` 40).
  - Drive f08-f15: the head is on the crowd line.
  - Mobile drive f06-f15: `emptyFgMean` 36.8.
  - Mobile c11: 53.5 px. Mobile c16: `emptyFgPct` 37.
- **Fix:**
  - **Follow:** a handler dead zone (x 20-80%) that also runs during drives.
  - **Shots:** pitch down 3-5° on a shot.
  - **Portrait:** follow the ball carrier toward the attacked basket during drives and keep the bottom band under 20% empty. Use the c02-c07 framing (80-88 px) for c11/c16 situations.
  - **Tests:** extend the safe-area test to bursts (drive `handlerInBandPct` ≥ 90, mobile `emptyFgMean` ≤ 20).

### 6. Make-specific crowd and the shot animation
- **Moves:** Animation.
- **Evidence:** c07 x 1090-1280, y 240-460 (arms up on an airball); jumper f08-f17; drive f05-f15.
- **Fix:**
  - **Crowd:** a home-section stand plus wave only on a home make; seated or hands-on-heads on a miss.
  - **Jumper:** a knee load before the rise, a held wrist follow-through, and a landing compression.

### 7. Front-end layout and small text (Blocker 4)
- **Moves:** UI, Typography.
- **Evidence:**
  - c13 and mobile c13.
  - The c14 dimmed live bug (x 360-920, y 10-85).
  - Mobile STEAL/JUMP (x 440-690, y 1500-1585).
  - The c20 pills and source line.
  - The c08 "FOULSBONUS" collision.
- **Fix:**
  - **Select:** a two-step Home then Away select with a persistent start action.
  - **Champion:** hide the live bug on the champion and summary screens.
  - **Size floor:** every functional label at 12 css px or more.
  - **In-world scoreboard:** school abbreviations, with BONUS moved below FOULS.
  - **Title:** frame the title wordmark fully.

### 8. Lighting beyond hierarchy
- **Moves:** Lighting, Art.
- **Evidence:** c02 and c16 players have no rim light; c21 skin and cloth render alike; there are no floor light pools; bloom is digits only.
- **Fix:**
  - **Players:** a key light plus a cool rim term on players only.
  - **Floor:** 4-6 baked soft light pools.
  - **Materials:** distinct specular for skin, cloth and wood.
  - **Glow:** extend bloom to an arena ribbon and exit signs.

### 9. Enclosure and place identity
- **Moves:** Authenticity, Art.
- **Evidence:**
  - c02 and c15 have no team on the bench.
  - There are no desktop title banners.
  - c09 has no ribbon.
  - The c02 crowd is orange, not gold; the c10 crowd is navy, not purple.
  - The c12 crowd stands on a ledge.
- **Fix:**
  - **Benches:** 5 seated players in warm-ups plus a coach per team. Remove the street-clothes sprites from the bench row.
  - **Banners:** 3-5 title banners on the desktop far wall, below the HUD.
  - **Arena:** an emissive ribbon on the c09 fascia.
  - **Palettes:** take crowd tints from the select-screen palette table.
  - **Hot Streak:** a 3-step bleacher and ball racks.

### 10. Mobile anti-aliasing and crowd alpha edges
- **Moves:** Mobile, Art.
- **Evidence:** my mobile c11 zoom (ring and jersey stair-steps); the pale fringes on crowd cut-outs (c05 zoom, around the heads).
- **Fix:**
  - **Anti-aliasing:** FXAA or SMAA after tonemapping, and at least 1.5x DPR on capable phones.
  - **Crowd edges:** premultiplied alpha with 1 px dilation on the crowd atlas.
