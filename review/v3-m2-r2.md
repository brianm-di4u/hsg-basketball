## 1. Verdict

The developer took the r1 verdict seriously, and most of it shows in the pixels:
- **Four r1 artifacts are gone,** each by its own pass test:
  - the pale disc over the ball in the net: c18 strands over the ball average (178,149,128), against (178,155,137) to (182,160,144) elsewhere on the net
  - the floor smudge: clean wood around the ball in make-wide f04-f06
  - the red sliver on mobile
  - the grade pop: the largest open-play step is 0.8% luma
- **The replay frames #2, the ball and the basket** inside the letterbox on both viewports.
- **The slam card sits in the crowd band on desktop,** clear of the ball and every player, and holds still after a short slide-in.
- **Venue and HUD:** the state-finals arena has its fascia ribbon, crowds wear school colours, the mobile STEAL/JUMP labels grew to about a 12 px font, and the champion screen lost the dimmed live bug.

What keeps the score at 6.6 rather than 7 is mostly new artifacts, plus three old items that are still open:
- **New effects bring artifacts of their own:**
  - The new rim/specular light makes dark-kit players in the unlit apron glow like ghosts, with a white hot spot at the hip (c07, c02, c06).
  - The ball-dim halo now paints a black blot on a flat wall in the mobile replay (mobile c08).
  - The title screen opens on giant, upscaled photo cut-outs with stair-stepped edges (c01, both viewports).
- **Still open:**
  - On mobile, the card covers the opponent's ball carrier (mobile make-wide f07), and it lingers into resumed play (c16).
  - Desktop Select still overflows (c13).
  - The athletes are unchanged mannequins in front of a photographic crowd. That belongs to later milestones.

**Against the milestone targets:**
- **Pass:** Lighting (6.5) and Animation (6.5).
- **Miss:** overall (6.6 against 7.0), Readability (7.0 against 7.5) and UI (6.5 against 6.8).

There are 3 blockers.

### Rulings on disputed claims

1. **Blocker 1: "closed" (A), "mostly closed" (D) or "partly" (B)?** Partly, as B said.
   - **Desktop passes on placement.** The card stays in the crowd band in c06, c16 and make-wide f00-f11.
   - **It does not hop.** Its left edge moves from about 237 to about 113 (cell-relative) over f00-f02, then holds at about 82-86 px through f09. That is a 10-tick entrance tween, not the mid-life jump r1 cited. D is upheld against B's "jumps/travels".
   - **Mobile fails.** In my 3x zoom of mobile make-wide f07, the card's top edge cuts across the opponent ball carrier (#42) below the waist: the ball and head show above the card, and a foot shows below its subtitle. In f06 the card sits just above two players' heads, and in f08 a player's feet touch its top edge.
   - **The lifecycle misses the test.** The card is at full opacity through f09 on both viewports, with the opponent already advancing in f07, and it is gone only at f12. That is about 0.4 s of resumed play, and c16 shows it at about half opacity 0.8 s after the make.
2. **The rim light: "the light r1 asked for" (D) or a "ghost outline" artifact (A)?** Both are right, and it is still an artifact.
   - In my zooms of c07 (x 350-480, y 340-510), c02 (x 930-1020, y 245-380) and c06 (x 20-180, y 420-720), near-black figures in the unlit apron carry a hard white edge and a bright white bloom at the hip and thigh. It reads as a glowing ghost, not as rim light.
   - A's "blown/clipped" c08 arm is overstated: 0 pixels reach 250 on all channels in x 830-900, y 500-580. It is still a near-white glint, too hot for skin.
   - The direction is right, and the execution fails the 7 anchor's "no artifacts".
3. **The mobile STEAL/JUMP size.** B is upheld.
   - The caps measure 17 device px (8.5 css px) in both mobile c02 and c05, which implies roughly a 12 css px font.
   - Before-after `mobile-01` and `mobile-02` show the r1 labels at about half that size.
   - D's "typography is not improved" is contradicted by the before/after. The labels now sit at the floor, not under it.
4. **The pale disc in the net.** Closed.
   - Net-strand pixels inside the ball circle average (178,149,128). Below the ball they average (178,155,137), on the left (178,160,148) and on the right (182,160,144).
   - My 3x zoom shows orange ball through an even cream lattice. The bright ring is the rim flash on the rim, which is Keep item 7.
5. **The grade pop.** Closed.
   - The largest consecutive step in desktop open-play is −0.8% luma (f12 to f13) and ±0.3% saturation. It is a smooth drift from 75.9 to 73.5 over 16 frames.
   - B's "unverified" is settled by this measurement.
6. **The floor smudge.** Closed on the floor. My 4x zooms of make-wide f00 and f04-f06 show an outlined ball on clean grain. The same mechanism now blots the mobile replay wall (mobile c08, and faintly mobile c05 x 320-410, y 320-420), and that is ruled a new artifact.
7. **c05 `ballContrast` 5.02 → 2.91: a Keep-item regression?** Not a readability regression in the pixels.
   - My c05 zoom shows the orange ball with a crisp dark outline, popping against the crowd. The number fell because the dim halo is mostly gone on desktop, which r1 ordered restricted.
   - The amended Keep item says the outline stays, and the halo is used only over the crowd.
8. **Probes that measure the wrong thing:**
   - **Desktop jumper `emptyFgMean` 8.5** measures the lowest player (the far-left group at the bottom). The shooter sits at the crowd line with about 55% of the frame as bare floor below him (jumper f04-f17). The probe needs a shooter-relative measure.
   - **c06, c18 and make-wide `ballContrast`** measure a ball in the net or a dead ball.
   - **c08/c17 and c15 `floorCrowd`** measure replay or close framing.
   - **c14 and c22 player numbers** measure players behind modal menus.
   - **Mobile c06 `emptyFgPct` 27.9** includes floor the card now occupies on purpose.
9. **The replay.** Closed for containment. In c08, the ball is at x 630-668, y 205-242, about 150 px under the letterbox, with the shooter and the basket in frame, and mobile c08/c17 are the same. Open for storytelling: c17 and c08 are the same rising-ball beat.
10. **The c01 title foreground.** Upheld (A, B and D's concession). In full-size c01, the faces at x 120-310, y 555-800 and x 980-1180, y 560-800 are upscaled roughly 4x with stair-stepped alpha. Mobile c01 has a blurred giant face at the bottom. It is a regression on the first screen a player sees.
11. **Mobile Select.** Partly fixed.
    - TIP OFF is in view (D upheld).
    - The Difficulty row is clipped by the footer (mobile c13 y 1475-1530).
    - Each team list shows 2 of 7 schools, with the cards cut at x 730 and no scroll affordance.
12. **The airball crowd.** Partly fixed.
    - The desktop c07 stands are mostly seated and clapping, with a few arms up (x 190-230, y 225-260).
    - The mobile baseline section raises its arms in c05 (in flight), c06 (make) and c07 (airball) alike. On mobile, a miss and a make still look the same.

## 5. Ranked fix list (ordered by visual impact)

### Fix in this milestone (camera, HUD, lighting, menus, artifacts, venue dressing)

#### 1. Clear the new artifacts (Blocker 2)
- **Moves:** Lighting, Art, UI, Mobile.
- **Problem:** the new rim light, the dim halo and the title foreground each add an artifact.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 2.
- **Fix:**
  - **Rim term:** multiply it by received light (or a lit-court mask) so a body in the unlit apron gets none. Tint it cool (about #9fb8ff), cap it at about 0.35 of the key, and clamp the specular so skin never clips.
  - **Ball dim:** apply it only through the crowd sprites' alpha, never on walls, floor or glass. Disable it in replay cameras.
  - **Title:** pull the attract camera back so no crowd sprite is shown above native resolution, or blur the foreground as depth of field. Use premultiplied alpha with 1 px dilation.

#### 2. Callout slot and lifecycle (Blocker 1)
- **Moves:** UI, Readability, Mobile.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 1.
- **Fix:**
  - **Slot:** pick it at spawn from candidate slots tested against the projected player boxes and the ball, with 16-24 px of margin, re-tested over the card's whole life using predicted positions. On mobile, prefer the upper band under the "+3" or the empty half of the court away from the inbound.
  - **Fade:** start it on the inbound or possession change, and finish within 0.3 s.
  - **Subtitle:** drop it, or shift the card up, when the table's wordmark is on screen (c16 x 305-520, y 283-325).

#### 3. Select layout (Blocker 3)
- **Moves:** UI, Typography, Mobile.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 3.
- **Fix:**
  - **Desktop:** use the spare width. Put Home and Away side by side as compact 2-column grids (smaller cards, no colour-name lines), with the options in a right-hand column, and reserve the footer height in the layout.
  - **Mobile:** a two-step flow (Home, then Away, then options), or a vertical list with a visible scroll cue, keeping the sticky TIP OFF.

#### 4. Shot camera and portrait framing
- **Moves:** Readability, Mobile.
- **Problem:** the largest in-milestone lever toward Readability 7.5.
- **Evidence:**
  - The desktop jumper shooter is at the crowd line with about 55% of the frame as bare floor below (jumper f04-f17; drive f08-f15; `emptyFgMean` 25.7 on the drive).
  - Mobile drive `emptyFgMean` 27.7.
  - Mobile c11: 50.6 px. Mobile c16: 41.5% empty. Mobile c09: 57.6 px.
- **Fix:**
  - **Shots:** on a shot, pitch down or dolly in so the shooter's feet sit at 55-65% of the play-area height and the rim stays in frame.
  - **Portrait:** use the c02-c07 framing (80-88 px) for transition, the arena and center-court situations, and follow the ball carrier toward the attacked basket.
  - **Probe:** add a shooter-relative empty-foreground measure, since the current one reads the lowest player (ruling 8).

#### 5. Replay beats and the portrait replay
- **Moves:** Animation, UI, Mobile.
- **Evidence:** c17 and c08 are the same rising-ball beat; mobile c08 uses only y 138-1285 of 1688.
- **Fix:**
  - **Beats:** make "early" the release, with the shooter large and the ball leaving the hand, and "late" the ball through the net with the rim flash.
  - **Portrait:** use a taller portrait crop (letterbox to about 80% of the height) rather than a landscape strip.

#### 6. HUD and type polish
- **Moves:** Typography, UI.
- **Evidence:** the c20 "next"/"rival" pills (x 1000-1040, y 430-447); "Press Space to skip" (c08 x 785-875, y 45-60 at sheet scale); the c16 subtitle collision.
- **Fix:**
  - **Size floor:** 12 css px for every functional label and pill.
  - **Mobile labels:** keep STEAL/JUMP at least where they are now (17 device px caps), and preferably 1-2 px larger.

#### 7. Lighting, the next step
- **Moves:** Lighting.
- **Evidence:** there are no floor light pools (c02, c19); skin and cloth render alike (c21); the net is a flat lattice (c18 zoom); bloom is limited to the digits, the ribbon and the rim flash.
- **Fix:**
  - **Floor:** 4-6 baked soft pools on the court.
  - **Materials:** a grazing-angle sheen on the wood; skin with a soft sheen; matte, slightly darker cloth.
  - **Net:** strand shading.
  - **Glow:** bloom on the c09 ribbon and the exit signs.

#### 8. Venue dressing and crowd sprite quality
- **Moves:** Authenticity, Art, Mobile.
- **Evidence:**
  - There are no title banners on the desktop home-gym walls (c02, c06, c10, c11, c19), though mobile c06 has them.
  - The same blonde sprite repeats across c02 (x 380-1000, y 90-300).
  - The Hot Streak crowd stands on a ledge (c12 x 220-1040, y 90-225).
  - Stair-steps on the mobile c11 ring and jersey; cut-out fringes on mobile c18.
- **Fix:**
  - **Banners:** 3-5 title banners on the desktop far wall, below the HUD bug.
  - **Repeats:** a seeded no-repeat-within-3-seats shuffle, with a mirror and ±8% tint jitter.
  - **Hot Streak:** a 3-step bleacher and two ball racks.
  - **Anti-aliasing:** FXAA or SMAA after tonemapping, and at least 1.5x DPR on capable phones.
  - **Crowd edges:** premultiplied alpha with 1 px dilation on the crowd atlas.
  - **Bench rows:** until the bench milestone, clear the street-clothes sprites out of the chairs (c02 x 505-840, y 295-355).
