## 2. Defects, ranked by frequency of player exposure

1. **Routine play still has mannequin motion and tangled bodies.** `bursts/desktop-open-play.png` f00–f15, lower-left paint, repeatedly stacks arms and torsos; mobile equivalent f00–f15 bottom play quadrant does the same. `desktop-jumper.png` f01–f07 center/right holds a nearly straight torso and small leg change; f08–f17 gives little readable landing compression. The ball genuinely leaves the hand (desktop drive f06–f10, center-right), so “no release” would be false. Fix the gather with a visible knee/hip load, asymmetric shooting-arm extension and held wrist, then a compressed landing; stagger off-ball spacing and poses. Foot contact cannot be measured reliably from these thumbnail strips: sliding is not established.

2. **Phone camera framing is inconsistent across common situations.** `bursts/mobile-drive.png` f06–f15 upper-middle play area leaves the shooter far away above long bare wood; probe `emptyFgMean` 27.687 exceeds the prior ≤20 target, though it improves from 36.8. `stills/mobile/c11-mountain-pine.png`, x300–480 y710–950, has only 50.6 CSS px median players. `c09-arena.png` has 57.6 CSS px and 31.3% empty foreground (wood y980–1300). `c16-crowd-reaction.png` has 41.5% empty foreground and 66.4 CSS px (wood/logo y830–1280). Follow the attacked half and constrain foreground independently of lateral handler position. The 100% mobile drive in-band probe settles horizontal containment, not apparent size or useful vertical composition. Keep the basket and thumb deck visible as in mobile c06.

3. **Crowd reaction still fails to distinguish a miss.** `stills/mobile/c07-miss.png`, x0–780 y335–485: raised arms surround an AIRBALL. Compare make `c06-make.png`, x0–780 y330–485: similar celebratory end section. The miss is not a rim-contact test, so absent miss rim shake is not a fair deduction. Tie the celebratory section to scoring outcome and show seated disappointment on the airball. Net and rim payoff deserve credit: both net-close strips f00–f05, upper-middle basket, show flash then ball stretching/exiting the mesh; f06–f11 show the ball below it.

4. **Make card placement is safer but lifecycle still fails the binding test.** `bursts/desktop-make-wide.png` f00 top-center places the card farther right than f01–f03, then it travels left through f04–f06; f07–f11 upper-left still carries it over resumed play. Mobile f00 has the card against the right boundary of the lower play quadrant; f01 relocates it inward. These are screen-space jumps/movement, regardless of cause. No current c06 ball intersection is visible: `stills/desktop/c06-make.png` card x275–660 y160–310 is well clear of net/ball x910–985 y450–545. Fix by reserving one viewport slot before appearance, locking it, and fading within 0.3 s of resumed possession. At five ticks per frame the f07-to-f11 span alone is about 0.33 s. Do not claim a full close on prior Blocker 1.

5. **Front-end clipping survives the new footer.** `stills/desktop/c13-select.png` has Quarters under the footer at y710–730, dim Shot clock at y750–780, and Difficulty off the bottom. `stills/mobile/c13-select.png` clips the difficulty buttons at y1530 behind the footer. Only two school cards per side are visible on phone (x50–730 y380–885), whereas the previous comparison exposed more choices. A scroll interaction is not demonstrated, so missing schools cannot be declared removed, but access is not readable here. Use the requested two-step selection or a clearly bounded scrolling list; reserve footer space in layout. Tip Off is now visible on both and must be retained. This is still a blocker against the whole-setup-fits pass test.

6. **Ball contrast effect is oversized in mobile replay.** `stills/mobile/c08-replay.png`, x300–500 y435–650, shows a black diffuse blot several ball diameters wide on a plain brown wall. `before-after/mobile-03.png`, replay before/after columns, makes the added blot obvious. Mask to a busy crowd background and shrink/fade the surround on plain walls. The probe's 2.13 contrast is not a quality score: it cannot distinguish an ugly contrast aid from useful separation.

7. **Character/crowd style mismatch and close-camera clipping remain.** `stills/desktop/c21-face-close.png`, x0–335 y375–630 has a huge cropped limb; x420–555 y390–635 has protruding jersey/decal geometry; hands at x830–970 y520–600 remain paddles. `stills/mobile/c21-face-close.png`, x80–300 y820–1060 shows the protruding chest graphic. Make hands and faces readable, bind decals to the torso, and widen close cameras until foreground limbs fit. Compare the coherent faces/hands and setting in `benchmarks/benchmarks-04.png`, Playgrounds middle-right panel. Crowd tint is progress, but photographic faces remain a different visual language (`desktop/c15-bench-close`, upper half).

8. **Small typography and edge quality remain polish debts.** `stills/desktop/c20-hub.png`, next/rival pill regions cited above, remain below the intended comfortable scale. On phone `stills/mobile/c11-mountain-pine.png`, ring x345–455 y880–950 and jersey edges x350–440 y740–900, show visible stair steps; `mobile/c18-net-close.png`, spectators x0–225 y380–700 show rough cutout edges. Enforce 12 CSS px for functional text and apply post-tonemap antialiasing plus improved atlas edges. Glyph height alone does not prove CSS font size; the current mobile JUMP/STEAL words no longer support the previous “8 CSS px” accusation.
