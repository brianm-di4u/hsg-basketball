## 1. Verdict

This round cleared most of what r2 bound it to, and the pixels show it:
- **All three r2 render artifacts are closed** by their own tests. There are no ghost-lit dark kits at the r2 coordinates (c07 x 375-455, y 360-500 peaks at 226 on the referee's white stripe; c02 x 950-1010, y 255-370 peaks at 212), no ball-dim blot on the mobile replay wall, and no upscaled cut-outs on the title (c01, both viewports).
- **The callout lifecycle is fixed.** The card slides in over f00-f02, fades over f03-f04 and is gone at f05 on both viewports, before possession resumes at f07. c16 is clean.
- **Desktop Select fits** 1280×800 with nothing behind the footer (before/after `desktop-07`).
- **The portrait replay** is a tall crop (about 86% of the height), and the bench chairs are cleared of street-clothes sprites.

The score does not move because the round also broke and exposed things:
- **The early replay beat lost its subject** on both viewports: c17 shows an empty half-court and crowd, and mobile c17 has no basket, player or ball at all.
- **Mobile Select got worse at rest.** The Difficulty row, partly visible in r2, is now entirely behind the footer (before/after `mobile-05`).
- **Dark-skinned players render as featureless black silhouettes.** The c16 head averages luma 1.2 (RGB 4,0,0) on the lit court beside a lit #14 at 59. Removing the rim term took away the last edge that modelled them (before/after `desktop-08`, 5x).
- **Nothing on the camera list was attempted,** for the second round running. Every framing probe is identical to r2.

**Against the milestone targets:**
- **Pass:** Lighting (6.5), UI (7.0) and Animation (6.5).
- **Miss:** overall (6.6 against 7.0) and Readability (7.0 against 7.5).

**Plateau: the stop rule triggers.** This round's overall is 6.625 against r2's 6.5625: a gain of +0.06, which is +0.0 at one decimal. That is under the 0.2 threshold, so milestone 2 has plateaued. The four blockers below are each small and well defined, and three of them were introduced or exposed by this round. Clear them in a closing pass so the milestone ends without regressions. The remaining gap to 7.0 sits mostly in Readability, and the camera work (fix 5) is the only in-milestone lever left that could move it.

There are 4 blockers.

### Rulings on disputed claims

1. **Dark skin "crushed to pure black" (A) against "only bodies off the lit court go dark" (D).** A is upheld.
   - **Measurements:** the c16 head (x 1135-1150, y 450-470) has mean luma 1.2, RGB (4,0,0). The player stands mid-court on lit wood, not in the apron. The lit #14 beside it measures 59. The c06 left head (x 63-80, y 445-470) measures 3.7. The c15 referee's head (x 860-960, y 90-200) measures 22, with a single white eye dot.
   - **My 3x zooms:** in c16 and c10 (YOU and #13), heads, arms and legs are flat black with no face and no terminator. In c06, the lit #12 and the YOU player have brown, modelled skin, while the player at far left is a black cut-out.
   - **Is it a regression?** Mostly not. The before/after `desktop-08` 5x zooms show the same player near-black in r2 too, but r2 kept a faint highlight on the crown and shoulder that is now gone. So the defect is pre-existing, and this round removed its last modelling.
   - **Ruling:** a lighting/material defect seen in most live frames. It fails the 7 anchor, hurts Readability (you can't tell which way a body faces), and is a representation problem in a game set in real schools. It is **Blocker 4**.
2. **r2 Blocker 2 (artifacts).** Closed, all three parts.

## 5. Ranked fix list (at most 10, ordered by visual impact)

### Fix in this milestone (camera, HUD, lighting, menus, artifacts, venue dressing)

#### 1. Dark-skin shading (Blocker 4)
- **Moves:** Lighting, Readability, Art.
- **Problem:** the darkest skin tone renders as a black hole with no face.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 4.
- **Fix:**
  - **Albedo:** floor the darkest skin albedo at about sRGB 55-75 instead of near-black.
  - **Diffuse:** add wrap diffuse (about 0.3) so the terminator softens.
  - **Rim term:** bring back the cool rim term at about 0.15-0.2 of the key, multiplied by the lit-court mask (r2's mask stays; the term itself comes back).
  - **Faces:** give eyes, brows and mouths at least 25 luma of value contrast.

#### 2. Replay beats (Blocker 3)
- **Moves:** Animation, UI, Mobile.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 3.
- **Fix:**
  - **Early camera:** anchor it to the shooter's position and facing at release, rather than to a fixed point.
  - **Late camera:** anchor it to the rim at the moment the ball passes through, and exclude any mesh within 0.5 m of the camera.
  - **Check:** assert in the capture that the projected ball and the shooter's box are inside the letterbox for both beats.

#### 3. Mobile Select (Blocker 2)
- **Moves:** UI, Mobile.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 2.
- **Fix:**
  - Reserve the footer height as padding at the end of the scrolling content.
  - Compact the option rows: put You play and Quarters on one row and Shot clock and Difficulty on the next, as on desktop.
  - Or split the screen into two steps (teams, then options).
  - Add a bottom fade plus a chevron as the vertical cue.

#### 4. Mobile card margin and the "+3" (Blocker 1)
- **Moves:** UI, Mobile.
- **Evidence:** Blocker 1.
- **Fix:**
  - Test candidate slots against the predicted silhouette bounds (feet included, not the probe box that stops above the shoe), with a 16-24 px margin over the card's whole life. Otherwise drop the card 24-32 px lower on mobile.
  - On desktop, delay the "+3" by the slide-in time, or offset it clear of the card.

#### 5. Shot camera and portrait framing (open for two rounds)
