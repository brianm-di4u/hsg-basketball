## 2. Defects, ranked by player exposure

1. **Routine shot framing still wastes the screen.** In `bursts/desktop-jumper.png` f08–f17, the shooter occupies the upper-middle band near the seating while bare wood fills much of the lower half. `bursts/mobile-drive.png` f06–f15 similarly leaves a long floor corridor beneath the shooter. Probe drive `emptyFgMean` remains 25.694 desktop / 27.687 mobile. The jumper's desktop 8.478 does not refute the shooter-relative problem: the metric measures below the lowest player, including trailing athletes. **Fix:** frame shooter feet around 55–65% of the play window, retaining the rim, and measure shooter-relative foreground separately. This is still the largest readability lever.

2. **Athletic motion has insufficient load and weight.** `bursts/desktop-drive.png` f05–f15, center shooter: the gather becomes an extended upright airborne hold; the available sequence offers little convincing knee loading or landing compression. `bursts/mobile-jumper.png` f08–f17, upper-middle shooter, does show a departing ball and return toward the floor, so “no release animation” would be false. `bursts/desktop-open-play.png` f00–f15, lower-left paint, and `bursts/mobile-open-play.png` f00–f15, lower play area, show crowded, spread-arm bodies rather than cleanly separated athletic silhouettes. Foot sliding cannot be isolated confidently from camera movement here. **Fix:** add knee/hip anticipation, wrist follow-through, planted-foot phases and landing compression; improve paint spacing. Model/mocap work belongs to the later milestone but continues to limit the score.

3. **Portrait scale is inconsistent in transitions and center-court play.** `stills/mobile/c11-mountain-pine.png`, x300–470 y700–950, compresses the matchup against a large logo with long unused foreground below. `stills/mobile/c16-crowd-reaction.png`, y820–1285, preserves the prior 41.5% empty-foreground issue. Probe c09 remains 57.6 px, c11 50.6 px, c16 66.4 px; compare typical c05 82.5 px. **Fix:** carry the typical live framing into these camera states. Keep the separate control deck shown in c05 y1290–1688.

4. **Replay camera loses its subject: new blocker recommendation.** `stills/mobile/c17-replay-early.png`, entire x0–780 y118–1570, contains wall, crowd and floor with no visible shooter, ball or basket. Desktop early replay places the hoop far left and clips players at the right edge (`stills/desktop/c17-replay-early.png`, x0–1280 y56–744). Late replay has the ball near the backboard but a large foreground referee/head hides much of #2 (`stills/mobile/c08-replay.png`, x400–780 y1080–1570; desktop c08 x700–950 y550–744). **Fix:** target actual release and net-entry beats from projected subject bounds, and exclude foreground occluders. Pass only when release shows shooter and separating ball and late shows ball through rim on both viewports.

5. **Mobile setup still hides functional options: carried blocker.** `stills/mobile/c13-select.png`, y1470–1688: Shot clock buttons meet the sticky footer; Difficulty is darkened behind it and its choices are cut by the bottom edge. Partial third school cards at x635–730 y380–1050 improve horizontal discoverability, but no clear vertical scroll instruction explains the obscured setup. `before-after/mobile-05.png`, first AFTER cell lower quarter, shows a worse initial options position than BEFORE. **Fix:** reserve footer height in the scrolling content, add a visible vertical cue and capture the final scrolled position showing Difficulty fully accessible. Retain visible TIP OFF.

6. **Small functional pills remain.** `stills/desktop/c20-hub.png`, x454–496 y466–485 and x1000–1042 y428–448, next/rival are much smaller than neighboring schedule text. **Fix:** enforce a 12 CSS px font floor on functional pills and helper prompts. The mobile STEAL/JUMP labels remain visually stronger than the pills (`stills/mobile/c05-flight.png`, bottom-right deck); do not shrink them to reclaim room.
