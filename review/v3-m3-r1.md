## 1. Verdict

The round fixed the worst thing the last verdict found and then shipped a shell the gameplay camera barely shows:
- **Dark skin is fixed.** The c16 player r3 measured at luma 1.2 now has a 59 shoulder and a 39 neck with a visible terminator. #13's face in c10 shows eyes, brows and a mouth. Light skin no longer renders as red plastic (c21). This is the biggest visual change in two rounds, and it lifts Lighting and Art.
- **The early replay has its subject back:** a whole, airborne #2 with the ball and basket in frame (c17, both viewports).
- **But the new shell is close to invisible in desktop live play.** The crowd sheet fills everything from the HUD down to the table in c02, c06, c10, c11, c16 and c19. Where the shell does show (c12, c18, the replays, the top of the mobile frames), it is flat paint bands: an orange stripe with blue clipped to 0, a cream band, no texture, no banners on any desktop wall. I can find no visible bounce, colour bleed, corner occlusion or light pool anywhere. The one baked effect the pixels show is a soft falloff across the replay's left wall (148 to 176 luma), and that wall is now brighter than the floor in both replay beats. The c18 brick wall became a flat orange plane.
- **Three of r3's four blockers are still open:**
  - the mobile card now touches #0's shoe (about 1 css px in c06, contact in make-wide f00)
  - mobile Select still draws team cards through and below its footer
  - c08 still has a head over the shooter and the ball above the rim
- **The skin-tone fix came with a regression:** several characters changed appearance outright, not just shading. The referee's black hair became grey and the c16 blue guard's dark skin and dark hair became pale skin and blonde hair. In c19 the visible LHHS team went from three dark-skinned players to none. In a game set in Hot Springs schools, that is a blocker.

**Against the milestone targets:** every target is missed, by wide margins:
- Lighting 7.0 against 7.5
- Art 6.5 against 7.2
- Authenticity 6.5 against 7.2
- overall 6.7 against 7.4

This is round 1 of the milestone, so no plateau rule applies. The gap is the milestone's own deliverable, the venue: get it into the frame, then make it read as built and lit.

There are 4 blockers.

### Rulings on disputed claims

1. **"The skin-tone mix shifted pale" (A) against "not supported; the range still runs dark to light" (D).** A is upheld in part.
   - **What is shading:** much of the change is the r3 red crush being undone. r3 rendered light skin as saturated orange-red: the referee is RGB orange in the BEFORE half of my 4x `desktop-09` crop, and D measured the r3 c21 skin at (58,3,1). Some r3 "dark" figures were light tones crushed.
   - **What is not shading:** characters whose hair changed too.
     - The c18 referee's hair goes from black to grey (`desktop-09`, my 4x crops of both halves).
     - The c16 blue guard (BEFORE about x 380, y 640-720 in the pair) goes from dark-brown skin and dark hair to pale skin and blonde hair (`desktop-08`, my 2.5x crops). Blonde hair rendered as blonde in r3 (c16 #14), so the r3 dark hair was not a crush.
     - In c19 (`desktop-10`, 3x), #42 and the ball handler were the darkest figures on the LHHS team in r3, darker than the YOU player beside them. Both are now light, and the visible LHHS team has no dark-skinned player.
   - **D's counter-examples are real:** the far c16 player, the c06 left player and c10 YOU are still dark. But the dark end is now thinly populated, and whole teams can render pale.
   - **Ruling:** a regression introduced by the Blocker 4 fix. It is **Blocker 4** below.
2. **r3 Blocker 4 (dark skin): "mostly closed, one face fails" (A, D) or "closure unproven" (B).** **Closed** on its pass test.
   - **Lit-side skin:** c16 shoulder 59.1 and neck 38.7 (my boxes match D's). c10 #13's face is 40-52. The c15 referee is 44 (D). The c06 left player's shoulder (x 50-62, y 500-530) is 43.4.
   - **The c06 face (23.4):** my 6x crop shows it in profile, turned away from the key and silhouetted against the apron. That is the shadow side, not the lit side the test names.
   - **Feature contrast** passes on c10 #13 (A 26-30, D 32) and on the far c16 face.
   - **No skin pixel exceeds 240,** and there is no dark-kit glow in c07 or c02.
   - **Still missing:** the cool rim term fix 1 asked for. No lit player shows a cool edge (c21, c16).
3. **The mobile card margin.** All three briefs agree, and my sample confirms it.
   - In mobile c06 the sole's last bright row is y 996, and the card starts at y 998 (x 555-580). That is 1 css px.
   - In mobile make-wide f00 the shoes sit on the card's top edge (my 2.5x crop). This is worse than r3.
4. **c17: "the ball just out of the hands" (D) or "the follow-through" (A, B).** A and B are upheld.
   - **Desktop:** the ball (x 650-695, y 180-225) is about 150 px above the raised hands.
   - **Mobile:** the ball (centre about 436,560) is about 240 device px above the hands (y about 800).
   - **Subject scale passes:** desktop 56% of the letterbox, mobile 50%. The beat is restored, just a few ticks late. Desktop #2's shoes are cut by the letterbox (y 745).
5. **c08.** It fails, as all three briefs agree. A brown-haired head (x 825-935, y 555-745) overlaps #2's head (x 795-850, y 528-590). The ball (x 607-638, y 308-338) is above the rim, not through it. In mobile c08 the referee fills the right foreground over #2.

   - My crop of y 1300-1688 shows the Lake Hamilton and Fountain Lake cards ghosting through the translucent footer (y 1400-1560), and the Cutter-Morning Star and Jessieville cards rendering below it (y 1640-1688).
   - The cue and the 2-column grid are real improvements, but the pass test's "nothing renders behind it" clause fails.
7. **The shell and "baked GI."**
   - **D's falloff is real:** the c17 cream wall runs 148 (x 120-160) to 176 (x 420-460).
   - **A is upheld on the substance:** no frame shows bounce light, colour bleed from the orange band onto the cream or the floor, contact occlusion where wall meets floor, or floor pools (c10, c11 and c16 floors are even end to end).
