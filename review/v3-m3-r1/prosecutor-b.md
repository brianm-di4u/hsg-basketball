## 2. Defects ranked by frequency of exposure

1. **Live play repeatedly collapses into a shared limb cluster.** Desktop c02 x155–410 y380–730 has overlapping torsos and arms; mobile c02 x200–730 y900–1260 has the same problem, and `bursts/mobile-open-play.png` f00–f15, lower-middle paint, sustains it. The YOU marker locates the controlled player but does not disentangle teammates. **Fix:** improve positional spacing and use pose/outline separation where bodies overlap. This moves Readability and Animation.

2. **Shot and drive framing spend screen area on floor while athletic detail recedes.** `bursts/desktop-jumper.png` f04–f17, shooter along the upper-middle crowd boundary and bare lower half; `bursts/mobile-drive.png` f06–f15, small shooter near upper-middle paint with a long bare floor below. Probe drive emptyFgMean is 25.694% desktop and 27.687% mobile; mobile drive ballContrastMin is 1.15. Mobile c11 has a 50.6 CSS px median player, and mobile c16 x0–780 y825–1285 devotes its foreground to floor/logo (probe 41.5%). **Fix:** tighten the action camera around handler, release and basket, with a minimum portrait player-size constraint. HandlerInBand=100% measures horizontal containment, not useful subject scale or empty floor; it cannot clear this complaint.

3. **Motion has the necessary events but insufficient weight and payoff.** `bursts/desktop-drive.png` f05–f15, central shooter, sustains a very upright airborne/release silhouette with little torso compression. Desktop jumper f01–f07, upper-middle shooter, shows a gather, so “no anticipation” would overstate the evidence; the criticism is limited pose range. Both net-close strips f00–f11, upper-middle hoop, show the ball moving through a basket whose net outline does not visibly stretch or recoil. Rim colour flashes over f00–f03, which deserves credit but is not net deformation. Mobile c06 and c07, spectator band x0–780 y320–480, both present raised-arm celebration imagery, diluting make/miss distinction. **Fix:** add a deeper load, wrist/torso follow-through and landing compression, deform the net during passage, and distinguish crowd reaction by outcome. These are visual animation recommendations, not claims about hidden animation code.

4. **Every make risks two unresolved overlay defects.** `bursts/desktop-make-wide.png` f00–f01, upper-right card/+3, visibly clips the tag against the arriving card. In `stills/mobile/c06-make.png`, right #0's trailing shoe ends around x575 y995, immediately above the card beginning around y998. Even allowing several pixels of estimation error, this is far below the required 32 device px (16 CSS px). Mobile make-wide f00–f02, lower-middle play area, repeats the near contact. The card fades over f02–f04 and is absent f05; keep that lifecycle. **Fix:** reserve silhouette bounds including shoes for the complete card trajectory and keep +3 in a separate tested slot.

5. **Replays still fail the requested release/make grammar.** Desktop c17 x635–920 y355–745 and mobile c17 x380–780 y780–1540 restore the shooter, but the ball is already well away from the hands (desktop x650–695 y180–225; mobile x405–470 y525–595). Mobile shooter height is approximately 750 device px, about 44% of the image, while desktop's visible silhouette is roughly 390 px, about 49%; scale is substantially addressed. Late c08 still has a ball above the rim rather than at/through it: desktop x605–640 y305–345 versus rim y397–414; mobile x345–387 y710–756 versus rim y840–855. Foreground heads overlap the shooter in desktop c08 x790–940 y530–745 and mobile c08 x610–780 y1020–1260. **Fix:** capture the release at separation and the late beat at actual net passage; exclude foreground bodies from that camera. Keep both basket and ball inside the letterbox, as these stills already do.

6. **Menu footer treatment still conceals real content.** Mobile c13 x50–730 y1395–1688 shows away-team cards continuing behind the translucent footer and reappearing below it, despite “More options below.” TIP OFF is visible, but footer space is not reserved in the rendered result. Mobile c20 x50–730 y1380–1510 similarly fades the fact/source into its footer; desktop c20 x240–1040 y665–710 makes the source particularly faint. **Fix:** clip the scrolling viewport above the footer and reserve its full height in layout; keep the scroll cue and visible action. This moves UI and Mobile.

7. **Small secondary typography remains below the promised floor.** Desktop c20 “next,” x1000–1043 y428–448, is markedly smaller than neighbouring schedule labels; mobile c06 subtitle x375–528 y1117–1140 is also small at 2×. Exact font size cannot be inferred from glyph height alone, but the desktop pill plainly warrants a minimum-size audit. **Fix:** enforce 12 CSS px minimum and sufficient contrast on pills, metadata and sources. Compare the deliberate display/body hierarchy in `benchmarks/benchmarks-02.png`, top-left/top-right Hoop Land menu tiles, central panels.
