## 1. Verdict

The venue finally shows its construction, and the players standing in it went dark again. Painted block with mortar courses now shows above the crowd (c02, c06), behind the c18 net (the r1 brick loss is reversed), and on the replay left wall with a green wainscot (c08, c17). The title has its first banners (c01: 1945 and 2000). Three UI fixes landed cleanly: the desktop "+3" is staggered clear of the card, c20 shows its Source line on both viewports, and the c08 replay has lost its head overlap and foreground referee. The r1 assignment regression is reversed for every character r1 named. But the shading half of the skin fix has reverted: dark-skinned players are black cutouts again (c16 YOU head median 0.0, the c15 referee 1.2, c10 #13's face 3.3, c19 2.8-10), and light skin is red plastic again (c21). That one regression costs Lighting, Art and Readability at once. The "baked lighting" shows up only as a screen-centred vignette: the same symmetric falloff appears in every camera, in two different venues, and on the title's vertical wall. The state-finals arena, the round's other deliverable, is structurally unchanged (c14 is identical to r1; before/after `desktop-07`). It is darker at the edges (c09 left floor columns 52 against 126 at centre) and still has a black void where its roof should be. The crowd got brighter, and the floor-to-crowd hierarchy and the ball outline both fell.

**Against the milestone targets:** every target is missed, by more than in r1:
- Lighting 6 against 7.5
- Art 6 against 7.2
- Authenticity 6.5 against 7.2
- overall 6.5 against 7.4

**Plateau rule: the round did not gain 0.2.** The overall went from 6.69 to 6.50, a change of **-0.19**. This is a decline, not a gain, so the stop condition is met. Nearly all of the loss traces to two regressions with clear pass tests: the skin shading (Blocker 1) and the crowd exposure (Blocker 2). The venue work itself moved forward slightly.

There are 5 blockers.

### Rulings on disputed claims

1. **Blocker 4, the assignment half: "restored" (B, D) or "swung rather than settled" (A).** The named characters are restored.
   - The c18 referee's hair is black again (`desktop-09` lower pair). The c16 blue guard is dark-skinned again (`desktop-08` lower pair). c19 #42 and the handler are dark again (D, `desktop-10`).
   - A is right that other characters changed the other way (c07 #12, c11, c12 #30, c17 #2, and c21 are now dark where r1 was light; `desktop-09` upper pair shows the c17 #2 change). Without r3 frames I can't confirm the full mapping. Light players remain (c16 #14 at luma 101, c19 #21).
   - **Ruling:** the assignment clause passes for every character r1 named. The shading clauses fail (ruling 2), so Blocker 4 carries forward as Blocker 1, scoped to shading only.

2. **"A centre-weighted falloff now shows" (D, credited as a possible bake) or "a screen-space vignette" (A).** A is upheld.
   - My 16-column floor profiles show the same symmetric bell in every camera: c02 81→135→90, c03 90→133→80, c11 101→144→101, c12 95→140→92, c16 89→136→84.
   - c12 is a different venue (Hot Streak) with a different camera, and the curve is the same.
   - **Decisive:** the c01 title's vertical banner wall (y 40-100) falls the same way, 48 at the left edge, 77 at centre and 42 at the right. A world-space floor lightmap can't darken a wall in screen-centred symmetry.
   - D's crowd counter-evidence is noise. My crowd bands swing 3-38 column to column with no trend, and a 30% vignette on a 25-luma crowd is invisible in that noise.
   - **Ruling:** no baked pool, bounce, bleed or occlusion is visible anywhere in the packet. The vignette itself is acceptable as a grade, but it is not GI.
   - **c09 is the exception:** it falls asymmetrically (52 at the left edge, 126 at right-centre), which darkens the arena's left third.

3. **Wall share of the c02/c19 frame: "about 10%" (A) or "about 5%" (D).** A is closer.
