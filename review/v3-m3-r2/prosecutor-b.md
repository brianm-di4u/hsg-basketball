## 2. Defects ranked by exposure

1. **Live-play dark silhouettes lose body-facing detail.** In `stills/desktop/c02-live.png`, x150–220/y410–600, the white uniform reads more clearly than head/arms; in `stills/desktop/c16-crowd-reaction.png`, x1070–1200/y440–650, the controlled player's face and arm merge into nearly black shapes. The new darker assignments are visible in `before-after/desktop-01.png`, lower pair, and `desktop-08.png`, lower pair (player regions). **Fix:** retain the restored assignments but lift lit-side skin and add restrained edge separation; verify the previous 35–75 luma and feature-contrast tests on actual skin masks. The supplied probe does not measure skin, so its whole-frame near-black percentage cannot settle that test. These are observations of lost modelling, not a claim that every dark pixel must be brightened.

2. **The phone camera wastes useful court scale in transition and remote play.** `bursts/mobile-drive.png`, f06–f15, central court: the shooter occupies a small upper-middle area above a large empty foreground. `stills/mobile/c11-mountain-pine.png`, x100–700/y350–540, puts most players at the far end while a broad floor field dominates y950–1280. Probe drive emptyFgMean is 27.687%; c11 playerPx is 50.6 and emptyFgPct 32.3; arena c09 is 57.6/31.3. **Fix:** in a subsequent framing pass, adapt camera distance and aim to the active possession while retaining basket visibility and the separate thumb deck. These framing gaps are carried, outside the prior verdict's milestone requirements.

3. **The make still has insufficient card clearance.** `stills/mobile/c06-make.png`, #0 shoe around x550–585/y960–985 versus card top y998, is approximately 14 device px (7 CSS px) apart, below 32 device px required. `bursts/mobile-make-wide.png`, f00–f04, lower-middle/right, confirms improved placement and an orderly fade, gone by f05; moving it did not establish the required still clearance. **Fix:** reserve 16 CSS px around the complete player silhouettes, including shoes, before placing the card. Keep its slide and fade. Desktop `bursts/desktop-make-wide.png`, f00–f02, upper callout/rim area, no longer shows the prior +3 being visibly covered; f00–f01 do not visibly present that label, so visibility of the label itself is not proven there.

4. **The shot payoff remains mechanically slight.** `bursts/desktop-drive.png` f00–f01, centre-to-right court, abruptly relocates the subject in screen space; f06–f15 holds a small upright release. `bursts/desktop-jumper.png` f01–f08, upper-middle shooter, shows gather, lift and ball departure, but limited knee/torso anticipation; f09–f17 retains a small follow-through. Mobile equivalents show the same limited pose vocabulary (same frame ranges, upper-middle). `bursts/desktop-net-close.png` and `mobile-net-close.png`, f00–f11, upper-middle net, show a descending ball and fading rim flash without an appreciable contact-driven net change. Crowd figures behind the net remain largely repeated poses. **Fix:** add knee loading, wrist follow-through, landing compression and net deformation, plus outcome-specific crowd motion. Foot sliding and physical input response cannot be reliably diagnosed from these sampled strips alone. Benchmark stills establish stronger posed silhouettes (`benchmarks/benchmarks-04.png`, middle-right and bottom-left), not superior timing: no benchmark motion is supplied.

5. **Replay beats fail the rendered composition test.** `stills/desktop/c17-replay-early.png`: player from y350 to below y744 letterbox boundary, shoes absent; ball x660–705/y185–230 is several ball diameters from either raised hand around y365–390. `stills/mobile/c17-replay-early.png`: both shoes now fit (roughly y1430–1495), but ball x416–484/y532–600 is far from hands near y790–850. **Fix:** choose a genuinely earlier release frame and separately fit the desktop shooter to 40–60% of letterbox height. Credit: `stills/desktop/c08-replay.png` and `stills/mobile/c08-replay.png`, basket/foreground quadrants, now remove head overlap and the foreground referee; ball lies at the net mouth below the rim's upper edge. No need to undo that improvement.

6. **Select footer still fails containment.** `stills/mobile/c13-select.png`, x50–730/y1644–1688: Cutter–Morning Star and Jessieville cards render below the Back/Tip Off footer. Above it, y1400–1448 shows the fading next card row. An opaque footer surface alone does not clip the scroll area. **Fix:** clip the list at the footer's top, reserve footer height, and retain the cue and Tip Off. `before-after/mobile-05.png`, first pair, bottom region, shows that below-footer escape persists.

7. **Secondary desktop type remains too small.** `stills/desktop/c20-hub.png`, schedule pills around y438/476, is visibly smaller than the main body labels. **Fix:** enforce a rendered 12 CSS px minimum for secondary labels and source/pill text, reallocating space as needed. No font-size metadata is supplied; the observed small glyphs support the readability concern, not an exact CSS declaration.
