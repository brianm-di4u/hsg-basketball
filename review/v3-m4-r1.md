## 1. Verdict

The mannequins are gone. In their place is a real character pipeline: c21 shows modelled brows, eyes with iris and sclera, a nose, ears, five-finger hands with knuckles, piped necklines and armholes, wristbands, and a "12" bound flat to the chest. At gameplay size (c02, my 2x crop) #53 still reads with eyes, brows and splayed fingers. The r2 skin crush is reversed: the c16 YOU head is 48.2 (r2: 0.0) and the c15 referee 65.2 (r2: 1.2).

But the round broke two things the binding verdict told it to keep, and the new models bring a set of artifacts that a stylized-film target can't carry:
- **Skin-tone assignment drift.** The models were swapped and the roster drifted lighter. The r2 pass test said "keep the r2 assignments", and Keep 6 said the same. Ruling 1 has the evidence.
- **The mobile card is back in contact with a shoe:** 0 px, against 7.5 css px in r2.
- **New artifacts:**
  - sneakers that are a skin over a bare foot, with five toe ridges showing
  - armhole piping that tears onto the arm when it bends
  - flat-topped hair solids
  - one face template with small eyes
  - officials dressed in the player tank top, shorts and wristbands
  - rust-orange dark skin with no blue at all, and paper-pale light skin in gameplay

The venue, lighting and animation are unchanged, as planned.

**Against the milestone targets:** every target is missed, by a wide margin:
- Art 6.5 against 7.8
- Authenticity 6.5 against 7.5
- overall 6.6 against 7.7

**Plateau check: the round did not gain 0.2.** The overall went from 6.50 to 6.63, a change of **+0.13**. That is a real gain, but it is short of 0.2. The player upgrade moved Art and Lighting half a step each. The artifacts and the drift held them there.

There are 5 milestone-4 blockers. Three milestone-3 blockers are still open and carried; they don't gate milestone 4 (see §4).

### Rulings on disputed claims

1. **Skin-tone assignment drift: "a regression" (A, B, D's Concession 1) or "better exposure explains it" (B's caveat).** The drift is a regression, and it fails r2 Keep 6.
   - **The before/after pairs show identical frames, with the same player in the same place, and a different skin tone:**
     - `desktop-10` upper pair (c19): the handler, YOU and #42 are all dark in BEFORE. In AFTER #42's face is (164,83,32), luma 102; the handler is (162,83,34), luma 101; and YOU is (192,118,68), luma 135 (my boxes).
     - `desktop-08` lower pair (c16): the blue team is mostly dark in BEFORE. In AFTER three of four are pale, for example the near blue player at (228,199,169). The referee is dark in BEFORE and light in AFTER.
     - `desktop-01` lower pair (c02): YOU and #53 are dark in BEFORE and light in AFTER.
     - `desktop-04` (c07 #12, the c07 referee, the c08 referee) and `desktop-09` (the c17 referee, the c18 referee) all go dark to light. c17 #2 goes from near-black to mid-brown.
   - **Exposure can't explain it.** The darkest tone now renders at a lit-side luma of 48-65 (c16 YOU, the c15 referee, c10 #13). The drifted characters sit at 101-135, the same band as the light players. They have changed tone class, not just exposure.
   - **What kept its tone:** the c15 referee, c16 YOU and c10 #13. These are the characters the r2 numeric clauses named, which suggests those clauses were tuned and the roster wasn't.
   - **What r2 Keep 6 named, and what happened to it:** the c18 referee keeps black hair but has light skin, the c19 handler and #42 are no longer dark, and only one dark blue player is left in c16.
   - **Ruling:** the assignment clause fails, and it goes into Blocker 1. The roster's balance has tilted visibly lighter in every gameplay frame. Of the officials the pairs show (c07, c08, c15, c16, c17, c18), only c15 is still dark.

2. **Toes through the shoes: "toes show through a black skin" (A) or "toe-sock shoes with sculpted grooves" (D).** These are the same observation, and it is a defect.

   - The white sole's top edge is torn and jagged on both shoes.
   - The left ankle has a white sock band, and the right has none; a white sliver runs down the right tights.
   - A sneaker has a toe box. Whether the foot mesh pokes through or the shoe was sculpted that way, the pixels show a foot, not a shoe. The owner's direction names sneakers.
   - **Ruling:** this is a milestone-4 defect. Blocker 2.

3. **Armhole piping on bent arms: "it tears onto the arm" (A), or not raised (B, D).** A is upheld.
