## 2. Defects, ranked by exposure

1. **Gameplay crowds still collapse into overlapping bodies; the new detail does not solve possession clarity.** In `stills/desktop/c02-live.png`, x150–425 y380–715, the white and burgundy players form one arm-and-leg cluster. In `stills/mobile/c02-live.png`, x240–730 y900–1240, overlapping white jerseys and limbs make individual silhouettes difficult to trace. Both open-play strips f00–f15 keep a dense cluster in the lower-left desktop / lower-center mobile regions. The probe's 100% handler-in-band for open play measures framing, not occlusion; its null ballContrast cannot establish ball visibility while held. **Fix:** spread off-ball positions, enforce body separation and give the ball/possessor an outline that survives bodies and jerseys. Validate at actual 390 CSS px width, not only the face camera. **Moves:** Readability, Mobile.

2. **Portrait framing squanders the model upgrade.** `stills/mobile/c11-mountain-pine.png`, x100–700 y350–535, reduces the distant lineup to tiny figures while y960–1285 is unused floor. c09's players similarly shrink (`stills/mobile/c09-arena.png`, x250–780 y450–960). Probe medians are 50.6 and 57.6 CSS px respectively; ordinary c02 is 83.2. In c16 the probe records 66.4 px and 41.5% empty foreground (`stills/mobile/c16-crowd-reaction.png`, y830–1285). Eyes and separated fingers cannot be read in these gameplay views, although both are clear in `stills/mobile/c21-face-close.png`, x360–770 y290–700. **Fix:** tighten the portrait camera around active play while keeping the rim in view; set a minimum gameplay body size and cap empty foreground. Preserve the separate thumb deck, visible at y1290–1688 in c16. **Moves:** Mobile, Art, Readability.

3. **Better anatomy is still driven through a limited, floating shot pose.** `bursts/desktop-jumper.png` f01–f07, upper-right shooter, shows little visible loading before lift; f08–f14 keeps the arms raised while the ball separates and travels. `bursts/desktop-drive.png` f06–f15, center-right shooter, holds the body and arms almost unchanged for 0.45 seconds. Mobile equivalents show the same pattern in the upper-center shooter. There is a release, so “ball never leaves hand” would be false. The strips do not establish precise foot sliding because the camera is moving; I do not charge that as proven. **Fix:** add a visible knee/hip load, elbow extension, wrist finish and an independently timed landing/recovery, even before mocap. Retain the clear release. **Moves:** Animation.

4. **The make has a flash but little physical payoff.** Both `bursts/desktop-net-close.png` and `bursts/mobile-net-close.png` f00–f11, upper-center basket, show the ball moving down through a net whose outline and weave remain fixed. The flash fades by f04; no convincing rim flex or net recoil follows. The crowd's already raised arms remain substantially the same through f00–f11, background center/right. That does not establish that no crowd reaction exists anywhere; it establishes no distinct contact-timed response in these samples. **Fix:** deform the net around ball passage, recoil afterward, add a small rim response and a delayed local crowd beat. **Moves:** Animation.

5. **Mobile make card touches a shoe, and +3 lingers over the backboard.** `stills/mobile/c06-make.png`, x560–595 y970–1008, places #0's white sole at the top edge of the card (card begins near y998). The new geometry consumes the old small gap; it is nowhere near the required 32 device px / 16 CSS px. `bursts/mobile-make-wide.png` f00, lower-right play area, also shows the card entering from the right with text clipped at the viewport edge; this may be an intentional slide, but it needs clearance throughout. f02–f13, upper-center basket, retains +3 over the top of the board, about 0.92 seconds from first to last sampled frame. It does not cover the rim. **Fix:** reserve a silhouette-aware callout lane, maintain 16 CSS px from shoes and ball through the slide, offset +3 beside the basket and fade it by f08. Preserve desktop stagger (`bursts/desktop-make-wide.png` f00–f06, upper half). **Moves:** UI, Mobile.

6. **Replay still misses the release beat and misdraws basket depth.** `stills/desktop/c17-replay-early.png`, x650–930 y180–745, has the ball several diameters from the hands and legs cut at the lower letterbox. Mobile c17 has shoes visible, but the ball at x420–485 y535–600 remains far from the hands, with the right arm cropped (`stills/mobile/c17-replay-early.png`, x420–780 y530–1500). In c08 the orange ball is a foreground disc over the front-left rim/net boundary (`stills/desktop/c08-replay.png`, x600–640 y347–388; mobile c08 x330–380 y775–820). **Fix:** capture the release earlier, frame the entire shooter, and correctly depth-occlude the ball with front rim and strands. **Moves:** Animation, Mobile, Readability.

7. **Select footer still leaks content and small desktop type still fails the scale.** `stills/mobile/c13-select.png`, x50–730 y1400–1688, shows a dim card behind the cue and more team cards below BACK/TIP OFF. `stills/desktop/c20-hub.png`, x760–1040 y425–490, retains tiny next/rival text. **Fix:** clip the scroll region above an opaque footer and reserve its height; enlarge small status labels to a legible 12 px minimum. **Moves:** UI, Typography, Mobile.

8. **Close-up hands and faces need finish, not just more polygons.** In `stills/desktop/c21-face-close.png`, x550–650 y150–435, the yellow wristband edge is visibly stepped; mobile c21 x450–555 y385–700 makes that edge particularly obvious. Both faces share a similar worried brow and small eye treatment (desktop x350–790 y125–340). Cloth is smooth across the torsos (x430–935 y350–790). These are materially better than the old mittens, but below the stylized appeal of the Playgrounds player (`benchmarks/benchmarks-04.png`, middle-right panel) and material separation of the 2K portraits (`benchmarks/benchmarks-03.png`, top row). **Fix:** smooth the wrist accessory boundary, develop stronger facial shape variation and broader eye readability, and give jersey fabric a distinct response. Do not compensate with realism-only texture density that disappears on the phone. **Moves:** Art, Lighting.

## 3. Regressions and previous binding issues

- **Confirmed regression: appearance continuity is not preserved.** The previous verdict explicitly required keeping assignments while repairing shading. `before-after/desktop-10.png`, upper pair, right-side handler/defender and left-side #42, changes visibly dark skin to lighter tones; `before-after/desktop-06.png`, upper pair, both midcourt players, and `before-after/desktop-04.png`, upper pair, #12, show similar changes. Current `stills/desktop/c19-handler.png`, x440–535 y340–500 and x770–920 y430–630, confirms the lighter result. Better exposure explains some change, but the packet does not prove these are the same underlying tones. Treat continuity as unverified and visibly changed, not as evidence of a roster bug. **Fix:** preserve each fictional player's assigned skin/hair identity across the model swap and provide matched skin-mask comparisons under the same lighting.

- **Confirmed regression: mobile card clearance worsened.** The old verdict measured a 15-device-pixel gap. `before-after/mobile-02.png`, rightmost c06 pair, lower-right player above the card, shows the larger new player consuming it. Current c06 x560–595 y970–1008 reaches the card edge. Carry the original 16 CSS px pass test forward.

- **Skin shading: visually much improved, full numerical pass unproven.** `before-after/desktop-11.png`, upper pair, restores face and hand volumes and removes the old near-camera black limb; `before-after/desktop-08.png`, lower pair, restores visible skin on the YOU player. Current desktop c16 x1100–1190 y445–540 is brown rather than the former black cutout. The supplied probe has no skin masks or cheek ratios, so it cannot certify the previous skin-specific luma/hue thresholds or continuity clause. Do not claim that blocker completely closed.

- **Crowd/ball hierarchy: partly improved, binding pass still fails.** Probe desktop c02 floorCrowd is 3.38, just under 3.4; c16 is 2.75 and c19 2.96. c04 ballContrast 4.13 misses 4.5; c05 3.73 passes 3.6; jumper minimum 2.08 misses 2.6. The crowded background remains visible above play (`stills/desktop/c02-live.png`, y60–300). These values support partial improvement, not closure.

- **Select and replay blockers remain open.** Footer leakage persists in `before-after/mobile-05.png`, first pair, bottom edge, and current mobile c13 y1400–1688. Replay c17 timing and desktop foot crop persist in `before-after/desktop-09.png`, upper pair, shooter/ball region; current desktop c17 x650–930 y180–745 confirms them. Mobile shoes are now inside the letterbox (current mobile c17 x510–735 y1390–1500), a partial improvement that does not fix release timing. c08 depth error remains as cited in defect 6.

- **Preserved:** desktop card/+3 lifecycle (`bursts/desktop-make-wide.png` f00–f06, upper half); complete mobile hub including Source (`stills/mobile/c20-hub.png`, x50–730 y1230–1660); kit separation and YOU ring (`stills/mobile/c02-live.png`, x325–435 y655–895). No blanket HUD or control-layout regression is supported by the pairs. The existing procedural motion is scored as shown, without demanding that milestone 5's mocap already be delivered.
