## 2. Defects, ranked by frequency of player exposure

1. **Every possession: crowded defensive poses erase body separation.** Four white defenders repeat the raised-arm stance and overlap visually in `stills/desktop/c19-handler.png`, x285–445 y390–640; the mobile equivalent packs overlapping arms and legs into x180–550 y900–1200. `bursts/desktop-open-play.png` f00–f15 lower-left and `mobile-open-play.png` f00–f15 lower-middle preserve that congested formation. This proves silhouette confusion, not continuous physical penetration in every frame. **Fix:** increase torso spacing for the larger bodies, vary defensive arm poses and prevent hands crossing neighbouring torsos. Preserve the clear YOU ring.

2. **Every shot: motion still lacks weight and finish.** `bursts/desktop-jumper.png` f01–f07 upper-middle shows the gather and rising body; f08–f14 shows release with limited wrist/torso change. `mobile-jumper.png` f08–f14 upper-middle confirms separation of ball and hand, so a claim that the ball never leaves the hand would be false. `desktop-drive.png` f06–f15 middle-right and `mobile-drive.png` f06–f15 upper-middle repeat a held shooting shape. **Fix:** in milestone 5 add a deeper knee load, coordinated extension, wrist finish and a distinct landing compression. Foot sliding cannot be measured reliably from these moving-camera thumbnails; I do not assert a measured slide speed.

3. **Every make: net payoff remains mechanical.** In both `bursts/desktop-net-close.png` and `mobile-net-close.png`, f00–f11 upper-middle, the ball traverses the net while its bottom opening and cone outline barely change. Rim flash is plainly present at f00–f03 and should be kept. The crowd is already posed in celebration throughout this short sample; this strip alone cannot establish a newly triggered crowd response. **Fix:** deform and recoil the net around the ball, with a short settling beat; add an independently readable crowd response in the later crowd milestone.

4. **Every mobile make: clearance improves but fails the binding margin; +3 persists.** `stills/mobile/c06-make.png` places the lowest white player's shoe near x520–545 y965–985, above the card beginning near y998 over the same x range. The visible gap is roughly 12–20 device px, below 32 device px/16 CSS px. `before-after/mobile-02.png`, rightmost make pair, lower-middle, shows the improvement from contact. `bursts/mobile-make-wide.png` f00–f04 lower-middle shows the card sliding/fading and f05 shows it gone. Its +3 stays above the backboard through f13 (upper-middle), long after the card clears. It overlaps the shot-clock/backboard area, not the rim itself. **Fix:** resolve the card rectangle against all silhouettes over its whole animation with at least 32 device px clearance; move +3 away from basket hardware and finish its fade earlier. Preserve desktop staggering (`desktop-make-wide.png`, f00–f06 upper half).

5. **During mobile camera exceptions: wasted floor and tiny bodies.** `stills/mobile/c11-mountain-pine.png`, x100–680 y350–1250, has a small far team and a long empty foreground; `c16-crowd-reaction.png`, x0–780 y830–1280, leaves a broad empty floor below play. Probe confirms c11 50.6 CSS px and c16 41.5% empty foreground. Normal play is better: c02 is 83.2 CSS px. Both drive strips retain the attacking basket in their shooting frames (`bursts/mobile-drive.png`, f05–f15 upper-middle), and controls remain below play. **Fix:** tighten these exceptional camera bounds while keeping the basket visible and the thumb deck separate.

6. **Every Select visit: footer containment is still broken.** `stills/mobile/c13-select.png`, x50–730 y1640–1688, exposes “Cutter-” and “Jessieville” beneath BACK/TIP OFF. `before-after/mobile-05.png`, left pair, bottom edge, shows no repair. **Fix:** clip the scroll viewport at an opaque footer and prevent list content from rendering below it. Keep the useful “More options below” cue.

7. **Close cameras: shoe and official defects remain conspicuous.** `stills/desktop/c15-bench-close.png`, x780–970 y640–750, still has ridges across the black shoe fronts, uneven sole edges and asymmetric white ankle coverage. Its x810–970 y380–700 shows shorts above narrow tights. `c18-net-close.png`, x375–510 y555–755, confirms the shorts/tights outfit; x360–510 y440–515 shows exposed shoulder caps despite the added sleeve pieces. **Fix:** author a separate rounded shoe shell and continuous midsole, symmetric socks, and an official body with a closed short-sleeved shirt and full trousers.

8. **Close characters still lack expressive identity.** `stills/desktop/c21-face-close.png`, x345–790 y105–340, shows larger readable eyes but two subdued, similar expressions; the incoming hand at x0–310 y0–190 distracts from the faces. `before-after/desktop-11.png`, top pair upper-left of AFTER, shows that foreground hand becoming substantially larger. **Fix:** provide character-specific brows/mouth shapes, taper hair silhouettes and hide camera-near limbs. This is below Playgrounds' pose/silhouette appeal (`benchmarks/benchmarks-04.png`, middle-right athlete and bottom-left airborne athlete); benchmark stills do not establish animation quality.
