## 1. Verdict

The body animation has genuinely moved from procedural to authored, and the bursts prove it. The jumper has a gather (jumper-close f01), a set point (f03-f07), a full-extension release with the wrist broken (f08), a held follow-through (f09-f15) and a landing with the ring back under the feet (f15-f17). The drive has a real run cycle with a planted lead foot, a trailing toe-off and a dribble that reaches the floor under the hand (drive-close f02-f06). The c19 handler crouches over a ball that sits under his palm, and the four c19 white defenders now hold four different arm angles, closing the cloned-forearm half of r2 fix 8. That clears the 7 anchor's "readable athletic motion (gather, release, landing)" clause, and Animation goes to **7**.

It does not reach 7.5, and the round brings a cluster of new, highly visible defects:
- **The dunk, the signature move, is broken.** The dunker's chest passes through the rim, with the ring wrapping his torso and the net drawn over it (dunk-close f06-f07). His body crosses the backboard (f08-f09). The ball is held off to the side, is out of both hands above the board at f10, and drops into the rim after he has gone under it (dunk f12-f13).
- **The net is still rigid** (net-close f00-f11), so the 7 anchor's net clause is still unmet.
- **The officials are frozen** in an arms-out bind pose for all 12 net-close frames.
- **The dribble layer disconnects the ball from the hand.** In c02 and c10 the ball is parked behind the far hip with the hand at the chest; in open-play-close f00-f01 it floats at the waist with no hand on it.

The art side regresses in four places:
- the armhole piping now flares off the shoulders as wings (c02, c06, c10)
- three hair buns float above their owners' heads (c19)
- c21 now frames two backs of heads
- c14 sits on giant pixelated fan cards over a navy void

The new replay effects blur the replay's own subject, and the replay now catches #2 with her arms down (c17). A "SLAM DUNK" card fires on a made three (make-wide f00-f04, both viewports).

**Against the targets:** both milestone targets are missed: Animation 7 against 7.5, overall 6.8 against 8.0.

**Plateau check: the round did not gain 0.2.** The overall is 54 / 8 = 6.75 against r2's 6.625, a change of **+0.125**. That is under the 0.2 plateau threshold. It is the first milestone-5 round, and the whole gain is the one category the milestone targets.

There are **10 milestone-5 blockers** (§4). The open milestone-4 and milestone-3 blockers are carried as defects; they don't gate milestone 5.
