## Ranked defects and concrete fixes

Ordered by exposure in normal play, with signature-shot failures weighted above optional menus.

1. **Possession bodies merge in ordinary play.** `bursts/desktop-open-play-close.png` f05–f08, central torso/ball region: the defender's chest and reaching arm overlap the ball lane; wide `desktop-open-play.png` f00–f15, lower-left, keeps several athletes crowded into the paint. This is not proof of a ball-hand detachment: occlusion prevents that conclusion. **Fix:** enforce a small separation between roots, stagger off-ball cuts, and keep the dribble lane on the visible side during contact. Pass with individually traceable silhouettes and a visible ball at each bounce apex.

2. **The dunk remains a slow airborne tableau, and its inspection crop fails.** `bursts/desktop-dunk.png` f02–f13, right-middle: the dunker stays aloft with very similar bent legs while approaching the hoop; f14–f15 still do not show a landing. At three ticks/frame, f02–f13 span 0.55 s. `desktop-dunk-close.png` f08–f12, left edge, progressively crops away the athlete, leaving no useful hand-contact close-up at the decisive moment. I cannot use missing crop pixels as proof of clipping through the board. **Fix:** accelerate the approach-to-throw-down transition, key a clear downward arm drive, then a brief rim release and compressed landing. Track the athlete and rim together in the crop through landing. The Playgrounds pose in `benchmarks/benchmarks-04.png`, middle-right, has much stronger leg/torso opposition; its still does not establish timing.

3. **Mobile dunk camera still follows the wrong part of the court.** `bursts/mobile-dunk.png` f04–f13, upper-middle: the dunker is a small figure at the far rim while empty wood dominates the middle and lower play area. The separate thumb deck stays clear (bottom quarter). **Fix:** retarget and zoom to the dunker during gather, keep the rim and landing feet in view, and demonstrate the prior 60 CSS px minimum for the actual dunker. Probe `playerPxMedian:89.8` and `handlerInBandPct:100` do not establish this: other, nearer players occupy the camera target region. `emptyFgMean:57.75` reinforces the wasted composition.

4. **Net payoff still does not demonstrate the required stretch.** `bursts/desktop-net-close.png` f00–f05, upper-middle net: the orange ball descends through a largely unchanged taper; f06–f11 retain that taper after exit. Mobile counterpart f00–f05, upper-middle, makes the same shortfall visible. I do not see a defensible 15% length change or a clear lower-hem flare. The rim flash is visible f00–f03 and must stay. **Fix:** pin the top ring and visibly pull/flare the lower rings with the descending ball, then damp the recoil. Extend the capture beyond 0.5 s: twelve frames at two ticks/frame cover only about 0.37 s, insufficient to prove the complete settling test.

5. **Jumper anticipation remains too brief.** `bursts/desktop-jumper-close.png` f01, lower half, shows almost straight grounded legs; f02–f07, central body, bends the knees after lift and holds a similar set-point shape. f08–f15 supplies a readable wrist finish but little lower-body change. **Fix:** add the prior verdict's 6–9 tick grounded hip/knee dip and a visible landing compression. Preserve the release and follow-through; do not replace them with a generic cycle. Foot sliding and input latency are not established here.

6. **Replay sharpness improves, but its sampled beat still misses the shot.** `stills/desktop/c17-replay-early.png`, still, x710–901 y370–744: #2's jersey number is legible while the crowd is blurred, but the ball is a sliver at the right hip (x827–842 y543–585), with the arms below a shooting set point. Mobile c17, still, x487–766 y808–1523, shows the same pose. `before-after/desktop-09.png`, upper pair, confirms the old airborne-ball view has become this hip-ball view. **Fix:** bind the capture to the actual shot release event, sample 3–4 ticks before it, and focus both hands and ball. Preserve c08's rim/ball composition (`stills/desktop/c08-replay.png`, x556–665 y258–427). Exact comparison to milestone-4 edge sharpness cannot be verified from this packet.

7. **Armhole artifacts survive the camera repair.** Desktop c21, still, x818–895 y541–662, has an open triangular edge/panel under the near arm; mobile c21 x151–370 y773–1015 shows broad yellow material projecting into the arm opening. Desktop c17 x839–877 y476–513 shows a small green loop at the shoulder. **Fix:** keep the trim flush to the jersey across the crouch and raised-arm extremes; remove visible inner-face strips. The front-facing camera is an improvement, not evidence that the clothing test passed.

8. **Mobile payoff and menu polish remain unfinished.** `bursts/mobile-make-wide.png` f02–f13, upper-middle, keeps +3 on the backboard tower after the main card is gone at f05. `stills/mobile/c06-make.png`, still, x540–574 y969–1005, leaves only a narrow shoe-to-card gap, visibly below the required 32 device px. Mobile c13, still, x50–730 y1644–1688, leaks team rows below the fixed footer. **Fix:** move +3 beside the score bug and expire it by f08, reserve 32 device px above the card, and clip the scroll content at an opaque footer. Typography/menu fixes belong to milestone 7; the event label itself is repaired now.
