The milestone's main deliverable is real and visible in every gameplay frame:
- **The photo cards are gone.** In their place is a 3D house in each gym's colours: black and gold at Hot Springs, blue, gold and green at Lakeside, purple at Jessieville, red at Mountain Pine.

The 7 anchors for Art ("depth on all sides") and Authenticity (banners, benches, students, scorer's table) are now met.

The carried player defects are unchanged:
