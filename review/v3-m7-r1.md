## 1. Verdict

This round built a real presentation layer, and it did nothing else. In the pixels:
- **The type system is real.** A condensed display face (the headings, buttons and HOOPS lockup) sits beside a condensed text face, with five visible steps in desktop c20. On desktop, every UI caption I scanned has a 9 px cap or ascender, about 13 px type, so the stated floor holds there.
- **The desktop menus now stage posed players in tonight's kits.** In c13, #15 wears the selected Lakeside blue. In c14, two players celebrate on the state-finals star in front of a lit, arms-up bowl. That still is the most improved frame in the packet, and its `voidPct` falls from 14.6 to 9.1.
- **The mobile Select footer is closed** (mobile c13).

But:
- **The 3D game is the v3-m6-r2 game.** My diff puts every gameplay still within 0.2-2% of r2 (HUD type and noise). Every m6 blocker is still open, or only partly closed, in the same pixels:
  - the replay icosahedron
  - the clone benches
  - the officials in shorts over tights
  - the necklines and bald heads
  - the c21 surfaces
- **The new menu players put the weakest model in the most prominent frames:**
  - white flaps at both armholes (my c14 2x crop)
  - numbers blurred with a dark halo
  - slipper shoes
  - a shared A-pose idle in c13 and c20, cut by the frame edge and the card
- **On the phone, the round's headline feature is invisible.** The card covers the full width at a 10 css margin.
- **The new floor is broken on mobile.** The "HOT SPRINGS" subline under every made-basket callout is a 7 css cap, about 10 px type.

**The scores:**
- Typography rises a full point to **7.5**, and UI half a point to **7.5**. Everything else holds.
- The overall is 57.5 / 8 = **7.2** (7.1875).
- **The gate fails** on four of its five clauses: overall 7.2 against 8.5, and UI 7.5, Typography 7.5 and Mobile 7 against 8. Only "every category 7 or better" is met.

**Plateau:** this round gains **+0.1875** over v3-m6-r2's 7.0. That is under +0.2, and it is milestone 7's first round. If round 2 also gains under +0.2, milestone 7 closes on plateau.

Even a perfect presentation layer cannot reach 8.5 while Art, Lighting, Animation, Readability and Authenticity sit at 7-7.5. The remaining path runs through the 3D world.
