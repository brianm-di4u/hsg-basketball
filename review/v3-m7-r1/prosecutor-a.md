## 2. Defects, ranked by how often a player sees them

- **Evidence:**
  - **c02 (1.8x crop):** six white bench players at ≈560-810 × 285-365, one clap pose and one phase. The three cheerleaders to the left (≈265-395 × 275-370) share one arms-out pose.
  - **c11 (2.2x crop):** six identical claps at ≈20-280 × 330-415.

  - **c09 (3x crop):** four identical claps at ≈425-560 × 240-300.

- **Fix:** 4-6 seated variants (elbows on knees, leaning back, towel, single clap, turned to a neighbour), with a random phase, mirroring and ±10% rate. Two or three cheer routines with offset phases.

### D2. Mannequin player art now staged at hero size in menus (c13, c14, c20, every menu visit) — **new exposure**
- **Evidence:**
  - **c14 #2 (2x crop):** the jersey's side panels hang as white triangular flaps below each armhole (still ≈145-165 × 480-515 and 220-245 × 485-515).
  - The same flaps appear on c13 #2 (still ≈35-55 × 435-470) and c13 #15 (≈1135-1145 × 415-450, ≈1240-1255 × 430-460).
  - Numbers "2", "15" are soft, with a dark blur halo (c14 #15 ≈1065-1110 × 455-485).
  - The shorts show a white crotch gusset (c13 #2 ≈85-100 × 540-555).
  - The shorts trims step in 2-3 px stairs at 1x.
  - The shoes are rounded slipper blobs (c14 #2 ≈130-165 × 720-750).
  - Pale #15 in c13 (≈1160-1230 × 315-385) has the flat hair cap the m6 verdict flagged on the bench.
  - The c13/c20 idle is a near A-pose, arms hanging out from the body (c20 #15 crop).
- **Benchmarks:** nba2k25-2/-3 (benchmarks-02) and the Playgrounds 2 hero players carry real silhouette and cloth. These read as a default rig in a skin swap.
- **Fix:**
  - Close the jersey's side seam (weld the front and back panels below the armhole), and bake the piping into the texture.
  - Use 2x-res number decals with alpha.
  - A sculpted shoe with a sole.
  - Pose the menu players from the game's own stance set (ball on hip, ball spin, arms crossed), not a rest pose.
  - Frame them fully inside the frame and clear of the card. Today they are cut by the screen edge in c13 and c20.
