## 5. Ranked fix list (at most 10, ordered by what would most move the overall score)

The overall is an unweighted mean, and five categories sit at 7. The 8.5 target needs every category near 8.5, so the largest levers are the ones that lift the 7s. That means the character model and the motion, not more presentation work.

#### 1. Rebuild the player model once, and use it everywhere
- **Moves:** Art (7 → 8), Authenticity, UI, Mobile.
- **Problem:** one mannequin serves the gameplay players, the bench, the crowd and the menu heroes: flaps, halo numbers, slipper shoes, bald heads and stepped necklines (Blockers 1, 5 and 6).
- **Fix:**
  - Weld the jersey panels under the arm, and bake the piping and the neckline into the texture.
  - Number decals at 2x with clean alpha.
  - A soled shoe mesh.
  - At least 3 hair meshes and a skin ramp on every head.
  - A polygon offset for overlapping close-camera bodies.

#### 2. Motion with anticipation, contact and follow-through
- **Moves:** Animation (7 → 8), Readability.
- **Problem:** desktop-dunk is airborne after the score with no landing (f14-f15); jumper-close holds the arms up through f09-f16.
- **Fix:**
  - Release the dunk on ring contact, with the ball through within 3 ticks, a 4-8 tick hang, then a landing compression inside the burst.
  - Jumper arm recovery after f09.
  - A 2-4 tick crouch before every jump.
  - A rim and net kick on every make.

#### 3. Light that reads as a source
- **Moves:** Lighting (7 → 8).
- **Problem:** `clippedPct` 0 on every still; mobile `floorCrowd` 1.96-2.24; mobile c14 `voidPct` 5.1.
- **Fix:**
  - A threshold bloom on the rim flash, the ribbon boards, the scoreboard LEDs and the c14 truss.
  - A rim light on the players in the close cameras.
  - Dim the mobile crowd to match the desktop hierarchy (`floorCrowd` over 3).
  - Re-aim the mobile c14 camera so the bowl fills the top 330 px.

#### 4. A phone-first menu layer
- **Moves:** Mobile (7.5 → 8), UI (7.5 → 8).
- **Problem:** no staged player on any phone menu; the mobile c14 band and empty floor (Blockers 2 and 3).
- **Fix:**
  - Stage one hero above a shorter card (head and torso in the top 30%) on c13, c14 and c20.

  - Drop the flat band.
  - Offset the mobile c15 camera about 1 m toward the official.

#### 5. Separate the bodies in play

#### 6. Authored crowd and bench variety

- **Problem:** one clap family on every bench; twelve unitard V-pose clones on c14 (Blocker 7); gem pom-poms.

#### 7. Finish the officials

- **Fix:** UV-straight stripes on a yoke, and drive c15 from the idle and signal set (Blocker 4).

#### 8. Menu staging per screen, with motion

- **Fix:**
  - A distinct idle per menu (ball on hip, ball spin, arms crossed).

#### 9. HUD residuals
